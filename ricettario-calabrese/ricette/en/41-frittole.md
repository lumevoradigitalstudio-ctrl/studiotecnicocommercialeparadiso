# Frittole (pork cuts slow-cooked in their fat)

*Frittole · frìttuli · Reggio Calabria · winter*

Frittole are the dish of pig-butchering day around Reggio Calabria. The less prized parts of the animal, such as rind, ears, snout, trotters, ribs and trimmings, are cooked for hours in a large copper cauldron, in their own fat, with salt and bay. At the end they are soft, glossy and fragrant. They are eaten hot with bread, and meanwhile *curcùci*, the cracklings, form at the bottom of the cauldron (recipe 06). It is a winter dish, to be eaten in company. This recipe brings it into a home pot, for six people, with times and amounts that are easier to follow.

**Serves:** 6 · **Prep:** 1 hour · **Cook:** 3 hours · **Total:** about 4 hours · **Difficulty:** medium

## Ingredients

- 2.5 kg (5.5 lb) mixed pork cuts: rind, ears, snout, trotters cut in half, ribs, fatty meat trimmings (ask the butcher, already cleaned)
- 300 g (11 oz) lard (recipe 06) or diced pork fat
- 1 l (4¼ cups) water
- 30 g (1 oz) coarse salt
- 4 bay leaves
- 1 teaspoon black peppercorns
- 1-2 dried chillies, optional

**Equipment:** heavy-based stainless-steel or cast-iron pot of at least 8 l (8½ quarts); slotted spoon.

## Method

1. Check that all the cuts are well cleaned. Singe any remaining hairs on the rind over the gas flame and scrape it with a knife.
2. Wash everything in cold water.
3. Cut the large pieces into 5-8 cm (2-3¼ in) chunks.
4. Put the lard and water in the pot and turn the heat to medium.
5. When the lard has melted, add the toughest parts first (trotters, snout, ears), then the rind, ribs and trimmings.
6. Add the salt, bay, pepper and chilli.
7. Bring to a gentle boil, skim, then lower the heat and cover, leaving a gap.
8. Cook for 3 hours over low heat, turning every 20-30 minutes with a wooden spoon, so nothing sticks to the bottom. The water slowly evaporates and at the end the pieces cook in the fat.
9. The frittole are ready when the rind is soft and gelatinous and the meat comes off the bone.
10. Lift them out with the slotted spoon and serve hot, with home bread and a pinch of salt.
11. Strain the remaining fat: it can be used as lard for cooking.

## Tips
- The more varied the cuts, the richer the dish. If you don't like the most gelatinous parts, add more ribs and trimmings.
- Serve them as soon as they are lifted out: as they cool they become less tender.

## Common mistakes
- Don't leave the pot unattended: the parts with rind stick easily to the bottom.
- Don't stir with plastic spoons: hot fat ruins them.
- Keep children away from the pot: hot fat causes serious burns.

## Local variations
- In many areas they are eaten with a fried egg or with potatoes.
- They are kept in their fat and reheated over the following days.

## Storage
In the fridge, covered with their fat, for 5 days `[TO BE VERIFIED]`. Reheat in a pan over low heat.

## Substitutions
Pork cuts can be ordered from the butcher, especially in winter. Otherwise use just ribs, rind and jowl.

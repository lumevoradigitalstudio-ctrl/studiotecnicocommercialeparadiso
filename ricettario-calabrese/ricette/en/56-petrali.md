# Petrali (fig-filled Christmas pastries)

*Petrali · Reggio Calabria · Christmas*

Petrali are the Christmas sweets of Reggio homes. They are half-moons of shortcrust pastry with a dark, fragrant filling of dried figs, almonds, walnuts, raisins, honey and spices. The filling is made a few days ahead, so the flavours blend. Then the pastry rounds are cut, filled, closed and baked. Many families decorate them with a white icing and coloured sprinkles `[TO BE VERIFIED: how common]`. They are kept in a tin for the whole holiday season. This recipe makes about thirty, and they are worth making in company, because the work goes faster.

**Makes:** about 30 petrali · **Prep:** 1 hour · **Cook:** 20 minutes · **Rest:** 1 hour + 1 night · **Total:** about 2 hours 30 minutes · **Difficulty:** medium

## Ingredients

**For the filling**
- 300 g (11 oz) dried figs
- 100 g (3½ oz) toasted almonds
- 100 g (3½ oz) walnuts
- 50 g (2 oz) raisins
- 100 g (3½ oz) honey, or vincotto, or fig honey (recipe 16)
- grated zest of 1 unwaxed orange
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 pinch ground cloves
- 50 g (2 oz) chopped dark chocolate, optional `[TO BE VERIFIED]`

**For the pastry**
- 500 g (1.1 lb) Italian 00 flour, or plain/all-purpose flour
- 150 g (5½ oz) sugar
- 125 g (4½ oz) soft butter, or lard
- 2 medium eggs
- 8 g (0.3 oz) baking powder
- grated zest of 1 unwaxed lemon
- 1 pinch salt
- 2-3 tablespoons milk, if needed

**To decorate (optional)**
- 1 egg white, 150 g (5½ oz) icing sugar, coloured sprinkles

## Method

**The filling**
1. Remove the stems from the figs and cut them into pieces.
2. Coarsely chop the figs, almonds, walnuts and raisins with a knife or in short bursts in the food processor.
3. Mix with the honey, orange zest, cinnamon, cloves and chocolate.
4. Cover and leave in the fridge overnight, if you can.

**The pastry**
5. Work the butter and sugar with a fork until creamy.
6. Add the eggs, lemon zest and salt and mix.
7. Add the flour and baking powder and knead quickly, until the pastry is smooth. If it crumbles, add a spoonful of milk.
8. Wrap it in cling film and leave it to rest for 1 hour in the fridge.

**The petrali**
9. Heat the oven to 180 °C (355 °F), conventional (no fan). Line two baking trays with baking paper.
10. Roll the pastry out 4 mm (⅛ in) thick and cut 10 cm (4 in) rounds.
11. Put a spoonful of filling on one half of each round.
12. Fold into a half-moon and press the edges with the tines of a fork.
13. Put the petrali on the trays and bake for 18-20 minutes, until lightly golden.
14. Let them cool on a rack.
15. For the icing, beat the egg white with the icing sugar until thick and glossy. Brush it over the cold petrali and scatter the sprinkles over straight away. Leave to dry for 2 hours.

## Tips
- Re-roll the pastry scraps only once: overworked pastry turns hard.
- Petrali are better after 2-3 days.

## Common mistakes
- Don't overfill the rounds: the filling leaks during baking.
- Don't overbake them: they should stay pale.

## Local variations
- With cooked grape must instead of honey.
- Shaped as closed circles or little tarts, instead of half-moons.

## Storage
In a tin, in a cool, dry place, for 2-3 weeks.

## Substitutions
The ingredients are available everywhere. Instead of vincotto, chestnut honey.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
Gluten (flour), eggs, milk (butter), tree nuts (almonds, walnuts), sulphites (raisins, vincotto). If you use chocolate, check the label (it may contain milk and soya).

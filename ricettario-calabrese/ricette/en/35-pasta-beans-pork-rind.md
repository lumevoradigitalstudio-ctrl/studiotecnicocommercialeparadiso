# Pasta and beans with pork rind

*Pasta e fagioli con le cotiche · all of Calabria · winter*

When the pig is processed, the rind isn't thrown away: it is scraped, boiled and added to the beans. Pork rind gives beans a full flavour and a soft, almost creamy texture that no other ingredient can give. It is a winter dish, to simmer for a long time on the stove, as is done all over southern Italy with small differences from home to home. In Calabria chilli is gladly added, and the pasta is cooked directly in the soup. Fresh pork rind is sold by butchers. If you cure meat at home (chapter 1), it is what's left over from the pancetta and the fat.

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 30 minutes · **Cook:** 2 hours 30 minutes · **Rest:** 12 hours · **Difficulty:** easy

## Ingredients

- 300 g (11 oz) dried beans (cannellini or borlotti)
- 300 g (11 oz) fresh pork rind
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 1 onion, 1 celery stick, 1 carrot, chopped
- 1 garlic clove
- 200 g (7 oz) tinned plum tomatoes, or 3 tablespoons tomato sauce
- 1 dried chilli, optional
- 2 bay leaves
- 200 g (7 oz) short pasta (ditalini or mixed pasta)
- salt and pepper
- grated pecorino for serving, optional

## Method

1. The night before, soak the beans in plenty of cold water for 12 hours.
2. Scrape the rind well with a knife to remove any remaining hairs, then wash it.
3. Put it in a pot of cold water, bring to the boil and cook for 10 minutes. Drain and discard the water: this first boil removes fat and strong smells.
4. Cut it into strips 1 cm (⅜ in) wide and 4 cm (1½ in) long.
5. Drain the beans and put them in a large pot with the rind, the bay and 2.5 l (2¾ quarts) of cold water.
6. Bring to the boil, skim and cook over low heat, covered, for 1 hour 30 minutes.
7. Meanwhile heat the oil in a frying pan and cook the onion, celery, carrot and garlic over low heat for 10 minutes, until soft.
8. Add the tomato and chilli and cook for 5 minutes.
9. Pour the mixture into the pot of beans and cook for another 30-40 minutes, until the beans and rind are very soft.
10. Remove the bay. Crush a few beans against the side of the pot with a ladle, to make the soup creamier.
11. Add salt and the pasta. Cook it in the soup for the time on the packet, stirring often. Add hot water if it gets too thick.
12. Turn off the heat, leave for 5 minutes and serve with pepper, a drizzle of raw olive oil and pecorino, if you like.

## Tips
- The soup should be thick: it is even better the next day.
- In a pressure cooker, the beans and rind cook in about 40 minutes from when the valve starts hissing.

## Common mistakes
- Don't skip the first boil of the rind.
- Don't salt the beans at the start of cooking: the skins stay tough.

## Local variations
- With pieces of sausage or a ham bone.
- Without tomato, with more chilli.

## Storage
In the fridge for 3 days, better without the pasta. In the freezer, without pasta, for 3 months.

## Substitutions
Fresh pork rind is sold by butchers, sometimes already cleaned and rolled.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
Gluten (pasta), celery, milk (pecorino, if used).

## PDO and PGI
None.

## Wine
A **Savuto DOC red**.

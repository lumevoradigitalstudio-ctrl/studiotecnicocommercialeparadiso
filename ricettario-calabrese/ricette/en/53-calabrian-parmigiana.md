# Calabrian aubergine parmigiana

*Parmigiana di melanzane alla calabrese · all of Calabria · summer*

Parmigiana is made all over southern Italy, but the Calabrian one has something extra. Between the layers of fried aubergine and tomato sauce go slices of hard-boiled egg, soppressata and a local melting cheese, such as provola or caciocavallo silano, with grated pecorino. The result is a rich dish, almost a main course, made in summer when aubergines are cheap. Like every parmigiana, it is better warm or the next day, once the layers have settled. The aubergines are floured and fried. Some families add a little 'nduja to the sauce, for those who like heat.

**Serves:** 6 · **Prep:** 1 hour · **Cook:** 1 hour · **Rest:** 1 hour + 20 minutes · **Total:** about 3 hours 20 minutes · **Difficulty:** medium

## Ingredients

- 1.5 kg (3.3 lb) long aubergines
- 20 g (0.7 oz) coarse salt
- 80 g (3 oz) Italian 00 flour, or plain/all-purpose flour
- 700 ml (3 cups) oil for frying (olive oil or high-oleic sunflower oil)

**For the sauce**
- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- ½ onion, chopped
- 800 ml (3½ cups) tomato passata
- basil, salt

**For the layers**
- 4 hard-boiled eggs, sliced
- 150 g (5½ oz) thinly sliced soppressata
- 300 g (11 oz) semi-aged caciocavallo or provola, thinly sliced
- 80 g (3 oz) grated pecorino

**Equipment:** baking dish of about 25 × 30 cm (9¾ × 12 in).

## Method

1. Wash the aubergines and slice them lengthwise 5-6 mm (¼ in) thick.
2. Layer them in a colander, salting each layer. Put a plate with a weight on top and leave them to drain for 1 hour.
3. Meanwhile make the sauce: cook the onion in the oil over low heat for 5 minutes, add the passata and a pinch of salt, and cook for 20 minutes. At the end add the basil.
4. Rinse the aubergines quickly and dry them very well, one by one, with a tea towel.
5. Dust the slices with flour and shake off the excess.
6. Heat the oil to 175 °C (345 °F) and fry the aubergines a few at a time, 2 minutes per side, until golden. Drain on paper towels.
7. Heat the oven to 190 °C (375 °F), conventional (no fan).
8. Spread a few spoonfuls of sauce over the bottom of the dish.
9. Make a layer of aubergines, then scatter over sauce, a third of the eggs, soppressata and caciocavallo, and a little pecorino.
10. Repeat for two more layers.
11. Finish with a last layer of aubergines, the remaining sauce and the pecorino.
12. Bake for 35-40 minutes, until the edges bubble and the top is golden.
13. Leave to rest for at least 20 minutes before cutting.

## Tips
- Dry the aubergines well before flouring: water makes the oil spit.
- It can easily be made the day before and reheated in the oven.

## Common mistakes
- Don't skip the salting: the aubergines absorb less oil and the parmigiana doesn't turn watery.
- Don't cut it straight out of the oven: the layers slide.

## Local variations
- With a spoonful of 'nduja melted into the sauce.
- With cooked ham instead of soppressata.
- **Lighter version:** aubergines grilled or baked instead of fried.

## Storage
In the fridge, covered, for 3 days. It can be frozen in portions.

## Substitutions
Instead of caciocavallo, provola or scamorza. Instead of soppressata, a coarse-grained salami.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
Gluten (flour), eggs, milk (cheeses).

## PDO and PGI
Optional: **Caciocavallo Silano PDO**.

## Wine
A **Cirò Rosso DOC**.

# Tropea red onion frittata

*Frittata con la cipolla rossa di Tropea · Vibo Valentia, Tropea · spring-summer*

The red onion of Tropea is sweet, crisp and mild. It is often eaten raw, but cooked slowly in a pan it turns soft and almost caramelised. In this frittata it is the star: plenty of onion, few eggs, a little pecorino. It is supper for evenings when nobody feels like cooking, and a good lunch to take out between two slices of bread. The secret is to cook the onion patiently, over low heat, before adding the eggs. It is protected by a PGI, Cipolla Rossa di Tropea Calabria, grown along the Tyrrhenian coast across the provinces of Vibo Valentia, Cosenza and Catanzaro `[TO BE VERIFIED: area in the specification]`.

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 15 minutes · **Cook:** 30 minutes · **Total:** 45 minutes · **Difficulty:** easy

## Ingredients

- 500 g (1.1 lb) Tropea red onions, 3-4 medium
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 6 medium eggs
- 40 g (1½ oz) grated pecorino
- 1 tablespoon chopped parsley, optional
- salt and pepper, or a pinch of chilli

**Equipment:** 26 cm (10 in) non-stick frying pan with a lid, or a flat plate as wide as the pan.

## Method

1. Peel the onions and slice them thinly, 3-4 mm (⅛-⅛ in) thick.
2. Heat the oil in the pan over low heat and add the onions with a pinch of salt.
3. Cook them for 15-20 minutes, covered for the first 10, stirring now and then. They should turn soft, glossy and lightly golden, without burning.
4. Meanwhile beat the eggs in a bowl with the pecorino, parsley, salt and pepper.
5. Spread the onions over the bottom of the pan and pour the eggs over them.
6. Cook over low heat for 6-8 minutes without stirring, until the edges are set and the centre is still a little soft.
7. Put the lid or plate over the pan and turn the frittata over onto it.
8. Slide it back into the pan and cook the other side for 3-4 minutes.
9. Serve hot, warm or cold, cut into wedges.

## Tips
- Don't rush the onions: slow cooking is what makes them sweet.
- If you don't feel like flipping it, finish it in the oven at 180 °C (355 °F) for 8-10 minutes.

## Common mistakes
- Don't turn up the heat under the onions: they burn and turn bitter.
- Don't overcook the eggs: the frittata becomes dry.

## Local variations
- With a handful of thinly sliced potatoes cooked with the onions.
- With a little 'nduja melted into the onions, for those who like it hot.

## Storage
In the fridge, covered, for 2 days. It is good cold too.

## Substitutions
If you can't find Tropea onions, use a sweet red onion and add a pinch of sugar while it cooks.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
Eggs, milk (pecorino).

## PDO and PGI
**Cipolla Rossa di Tropea Calabria PGI**. Optional: **Pecorino del Monte Poro PDO** instead of ordinary pecorino.

## Wine
A **Cirò Bianco DOC**.

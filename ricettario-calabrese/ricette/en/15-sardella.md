# Sardella: its history and a version for today

*Sardella, rosamarina · Crotone, Ionian coast · all year*

On the Ionian coast around Crotone, near Crucoli and Cirò Marina, sardella was for generations a red, fiercely hot cream spread on bread. It was made in spring with *novellame*, newly hatched sardines, salted and worked with a great deal of chilli, then left to mature in jars. Today catching, keeping and selling novellame is banned, to protect young fish and the sea. Anyone selling "sardella" made with it is breaking the law. On this page the historical sardella is described, not prepared. The recipe is a version for today with salted adult anchovies, which gives a similar red, savoury cream while respecting the law.

**Makes:** about 2 jars of 200 ml (¾ cup) · **Prep:** 30 minutes · **Rest:** 1 day · **Difficulty:** easy

## The historical sardella

The traditional recipe combined sardine fry, salt and ground chilli. Regulation (EC) 1967/2006 sets minimum catch sizes in the Mediterranean. For some years Calabria had an exemption for novellame, which ended in 2010. Since then fishing for novellame has been illegal, and the Italian coastguard seizes the fish and fines those who catch or sell it.

## Ingredients (today's version)

- 200 g (7 oz) salted anchovy fillets, already cleaned (about 350 g (12 oz) if whole)
- 40 g (1½ oz) ground sweet Calabrian chilli
- 15-25 g (0.5-0.9 oz) ground hot Calabrian chilli
- 1 teaspoon crushed fennel seeds, optional
- 100 ml (½ cup) extra virgin olive oil, plus a little to cover

**Equipment:** mortar or blender, clean jars.

## Method

1. If the anchovies are whole, rinse them under cold water and remove head, backbone and tail.
2. Soak the fillets in cold water for 15 minutes to remove some of the salt.
3. Drain and dry them very well with paper towels.
4. Pound the anchovies in the mortar, or blend them in short bursts, until they become a paste.
5. Add both chillies and the fennel and work for 1 more minute.
6. Pour in the oil in a thin stream, stirring, until the cream is soft and glossy, dark red.
7. Taste and adjust the heat with the hot chilli.
8. Fill the jars, pressing down, and cover the surface with a film of oil.
9. Close and leave in the fridge for 1 day before serving.

## Tips
- Spread it thinly on warm or toasted bread: it is very strong.
- A teaspoon melted into hot olive oil makes a quick pasta sauce.

## Common mistakes
- Don't buy or use "sardella" made with novellame: it is illegal.
- Don't leave the jar out of the fridge.

## Local variations
- Some cooks use salted adult sardines instead of anchovies.

## Storage
In the fridge, always covered with a film of oil, within 2 weeks.

## Substitutions
Salted anchovies are sold in Italian delis everywhere. Well-drained anchovies in oil also work: in that case skip step 2.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
Fish (anchovies).

## PDO and PGI
None.

## Wine
A **Cirò Rosato DOC**, from the same coast.

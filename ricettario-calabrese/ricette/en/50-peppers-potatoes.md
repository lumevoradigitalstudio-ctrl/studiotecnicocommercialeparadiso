# Pipi e patate (fried peppers and potatoes)

*Pipi e patate · pipi e patati · all of Calabria · summer*

In summer, in Calabrian kitchens, the pan of peppers and potatoes is always on the stove. The dish is simple: potatoes and peppers fried together in olive oil, until the potatoes are golden at the edges and the peppers soft and sweet. They are eaten hot as a side dish, warm with bread and the next day even cold. It is also the dish people make when there is little in the house. Every family makes it its own way: some add onion, some a tomato, some crack two eggs into the pan at the end. Here you'll find the basic version, with the times to get it right.

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 15 minutes · **Cook:** 30 minutes · **Total:** 45 minutes · **Difficulty:** easy

## Ingredients

- 600 g (1.3 lb) yellow-fleshed potatoes
- 500 g (1.1 lb) sweet peppers, red and yellow, or long green frying peppers
- 1 small Tropea red onion, optional
- 150 ml (¾ cup) extra virgin olive oil, for a 28 cm (11 in) frying pan
- fine salt

## Method

1. Peel the potatoes, wash them and cut them into wedges 1 cm (⅜ in) thick.
2. Dry the potatoes very well with a tea towel: water makes the oil spit and stops the potatoes browning.
3. Wash the peppers, remove the stem, seeds and white membranes. Cut them into strips 2 cm (¾ in) wide and dry them well.
4. If using the onion, cut it into thin wedges.
5. Heat the oil in the pan over medium heat. It is ready when a piece of potato dropped in sizzles straight away around the edges (about 170 °C (340 °F)).
6. Add the potatoes in a single layer. Cook them for 10 minutes, without touching them for the first 4, then turn them: they should be golden on one side.
7. Add the peppers. Stir gently with a spatula, so the potatoes don't break.
8. Cook for 15 minutes, turning every 3-4 minutes. The peppers should wilt and take on a few dark spots, and the potatoes should become soft inside (test with a fork).
9. If using the onion, add it for the last 5 minutes, until soft and lightly coloured.
10. Salt only at the end and stir one last time.
11. Lift out with a slotted spoon and rest on paper towels for 1 minute, if you want to remove some oil. Serve hot or warm.

## Tips
- Use a wide pan: if the vegetables are crowded, they steam instead of frying.
- Salting at the end keeps the potatoes crisp outside.
- The leftover oil, strained, is excellent for dressing pasta the next day.

## Common mistakes
- Don't turn the potatoes too soon: they stick and break.
- Don't cover the pan: the steam softens everything and the dish turns into a stew.

## Local variations
- **With tomato:** in the last 5 minutes add 2 ripe tomatoes, chopped.
- **With eggs:** at the end of cooking crack 4 eggs into the pan and stir until they set (see also peppers and eggs, recipe 22).
- **Baked:** toss the raw vegetables with 4 tablespoons of oil and salt, and bake in a tray at 200 °C (390 °F) for 40-45 minutes, turning halfway.

## Storage
In the fridge, in a closed container, for 2 days. Reheat in a pan without adding oil.

## Substitutions
Any thin-walled sweet peppers work, including Italian frying peppers. The potatoes should be yellow-fleshed and suitable for frying, not floury.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
None.

## Wine
A young, fresh red, such as a **Lamezia DOC red**.

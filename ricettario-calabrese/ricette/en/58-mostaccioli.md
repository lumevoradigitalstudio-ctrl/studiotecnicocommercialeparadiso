# Mostaccioli of Soriano (honey biscuits)

*Mostaccioli di Soriano · 'nzuddha · Soriano Calabro, Vibo Valentia · fairs and feast days*

Mostaccioli come from Soriano Calabro, in the Serre hills near Vibo Valentia. They are hard, golden biscuits made with flour, honey and cooked grape must, with no eggs and no fat. They come in many shapes, such as fish, hearts, horses, baskets and figures of saints, and are sold at fairs and patron saints' festivals, often decorated with coloured paper or foil. They are sweets to keep and to give as gifts. Hard when fresh, they soften with time or are dipped in wine. This recipe makes them at home in simple shapes, cut by hand or with cutters. The dark colour comes from the honey and the cooked must.

**Makes:** about 20 mostaccioli · **Prep:** 45 minutes · **Cook:** 20 minutes · **Rest:** 1 night (optional) · **Total:** about 1 hour 15 minutes · **Difficulty:** medium

## Ingredients

- 500 g (1.1 lb) Italian 00 flour, or plain/all-purpose flour
- 250 g (9 oz) honey
- 80 ml (¼ cup) vincotto (cooked grape must) or water
- grated zest of 1 unwaxed orange
- 1 pinch ground cloves
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon, optional
- 3 g baker's ammonia (ammonium bicarbonate), or 5 g (0.2 oz) bicarbonate of soda

**Equipment:** rolling pin, sharp knife or cookie cutters, baking trays with baking paper.

## Method

1. Warm the honey with the vincotto in a small pan over low heat, until runny and hot, without letting it boil.
2. Dissolve the baker's ammonia in a spoonful of warm water.
3. In a bowl mix the flour, orange zest and spices.
4. Pour the hot honey over the flour and mix with a wooden spoon, then add the ammonia.
5. When the dough is lukewarm, knead it by hand on the work surface for 5 minutes, until smooth and firm.
6. If you have time, wrap it in cling film and leave it overnight at room temperature: it is easier to work.
7. Heat the oven to 180 °C (355 °F), conventional (no fan).
8. Roll the dough out 1 cm (⅜ in) thick on a floured surface.
9. Cut out shapes with the knife or cutters: fish, hearts, stars, figures.
10. Draw on the surface with the tip of the knife: fish scales, a border, small cuts.
11. Put the mostaccioli on the lined trays, spaced apart.
12. Bake for 15-20 minutes, until puffed and dark golden.
13. Let them cool on a rack: they harden as they cool.

## Tips
- Work the dough while still lukewarm: cold, it turns hard.
- Baker's ammonia gives off a strong smell while baking: that's normal, and it disappears completely once the biscuits are cold.

## Common mistakes
- Don't roll the dough too thin: the mostaccioli burn.
- Don't store them somewhere damp: they turn sticky.

## Local variations
- Decorated with coloured foil stuck on with a little honey, like the ones at fairs.

## Storage
In a tin, in a dry place, for months. They soften with time.

## Substitutions
Vincotto is sold in shops selling southern Italian products. Otherwise use water and add 50 g (2 oz) more honey. Baker's ammonia is sold in Italian, Greek and Scandinavian shops.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
Gluten (flour). Sulphites (vincotto, if used).

## PDO and PGI
None.

## Wine
To dip in a sweet wine, such as **Greco di Bianco DOC**.

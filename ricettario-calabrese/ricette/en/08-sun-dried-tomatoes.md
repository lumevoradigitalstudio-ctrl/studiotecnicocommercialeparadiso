# Sun-dried tomatoes in oil

*Pomodori secchi sott'olio · all of Calabria · summer*

In summer, on the flat roofs and balconies of Calabrian villages, halved tomatoes dry in the sun on wooden racks, covered with a net against the flies. In a few days they turn dark red, wrinkled and very sweet. They are kept just like that, dry, or under olive oil with oregano and chilli, ready for antipasti, for pasta with toasted breadcrumbs or for a sandwich. This page gives you two ways to dry them: in the sun or in a home oven. Once in oil, dried tomatoes must be acidified with vinegar and processed, just like other vegetables. Oil alone does not make them safe.

**Makes:** 3 jars of 250 ml (1 cup) · **Prep:** 1 hour · **Drying:** 4-6 days in the sun or 8-10 hours in the oven · **Difficulty:** medium

## Ingredients

- 3 kg (6.6 lb) ripe, firm tomatoes, small and elongated, such as San Marzano or long datterini
- 30 g (1 oz) fine salt
- 500 ml (2 cups) white wine vinegar with at least 5% acidity
- 500 ml (2 cups) water
- 1 tablespoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon dried chilli flakes
- about 400 ml (1¾ cups) extra virgin olive oil

**Equipment:** drying racks or oven trays with a wire rack, fine net (for the sun), 250 ml (1 cup) jars with new lids, tall pot for processing.

## Method

**Drying**
1. Wash and dry the tomatoes. Cut them in half lengthwise, without separating the two halves completely if they are small.
2. Scoop out the wettest seeds with a teaspoon, if there are a lot.
3. Lay the tomatoes on the racks cut side up and sprinkle them with salt.
4. **In the sun:** put them in full sun, covered with the net, and bring them in at night, because the night dew wets them. Turn them after the second day. They are ready in 4-6 days.
5. **In the oven:** dry them at 80 °C (176 °F), fan setting, with the door held ajar with a wooden spoon, for 8-10 hours.
6. They are ready when they are dry and flexible like leather, with no damp spots in the middle. When you bend them, no liquid should come out.

**The jar**
7. Bring the vinegar and water to the boil.
8. Plunge the dried tomatoes into the boiling liquid for 2-3 minutes, then drain.
9. Spread them on a clean tea towel and let them dry for 1-2 hours, until dry to the touch.
10. Season with oregano and chilli. Use only **dried** flavourings.
11. Fill the jars, pressing with a spoon, up to 2 cm (¾ in) below the rim.
12. Cover with oil. Wait 30 minutes for the oil to settle into the gaps and top up to 1-2 cm (⅜-¾ in) below the rim.
13. Wipe the rims and close with the new lids.
14. Put the jars in the tall pot, covered by a few centimetres of cold water, separated by a tea towel.
15. Bring to a rolling boil and from that moment count **at least 15 minutes** `[TO BE VERIFIED: ISS time for 250 ml (1 cup) jars]`. If the boil stops, start counting again from zero.
16. Let them cool in the water, then dry the jars. Check them for 10-15 days: the oil must stay clear, with no bubbles.

## Tips
- Small, elongated tomatoes dry faster and more evenly.
- Sun-drying needs hot, dry, breezy days. In humid weather, use the oven.

## Common mistakes
- Don't put garlic, basil or fresh herbs in the jar: they are a risk. Use only dried flavourings.
- Don't seal the tomatoes while still wet with vinegar: the water makes the oil cloudy.

## When to throw a jar away
Throw it away **without tasting** if the lid is bulging, if you see bubbles, cloudy oil or foam, or if there is a hiss of gas or a strange smell when you open it.

## Local variations
- Many families keep the tomatoes simply dried, in cloth bags, and soak them in water and vinegar when needed.
- Some add desalted capers or anchovy fillets `[TO BE VERIFIED: safety of these additions in the preserve]`. If you want them, add them only when serving.

## Storage
- Dried tomatoes only: in a cloth bag or a closed jar, in the dark, for a few months.
- Processed jars in oil: in a cool, dark place, within the year `[TO BE VERIFIED]`.
- Opened jar: in the fridge, always covered with oil, within 7-10 days.

## Substitutions
Any small sauce tomato works. A fan oven works anywhere.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
Sulphites, possible in wine vinegar.

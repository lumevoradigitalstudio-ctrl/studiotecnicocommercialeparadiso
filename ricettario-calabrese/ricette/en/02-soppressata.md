# Soppressata

*Soppressata · all of Calabria · winter*

Soppressata is the salume for special occasions, the one that gets sliced when a guest arrives. It is made from the leanest parts of the pig, leg and shoulder, with a little diced back fat. The meat is cut coarsely, seasoned with salt, whole black peppercorns and chilli, and stuffed tightly. Then it is pressed between two boards, which gives it its slightly flat shape and its name. A slice shows the coarse grain, the red of the meat and the white of the fat. Of all home-cured meats it takes longest to cure, and it is the most satisfying to cut after two months of waiting.

**Makes:** 4-5 soppressate, about 1.3 kg (2.9 lb) · **Prep:** 2 hours · **Rest:** 48 hours + 7-10 weeks · **Difficulty:** challenging

Before you start, read the **safety rules** at the beginning of this chapter.

## Ingredients

**For 2 kg (4.4 lb) of mixture**
- 1.8 kg (4 lb) lean pork leg and shoulder, sinews removed
- 200 g (7 oz) firm back fat, that is 10%; the PDO specification allows 4 to 15%
- 56 g (2 oz) salt in total, that is 2.8%, including the curing salt calculated as at the start of the chapter, limit 80 mg/kg
- 6 g (0.2 oz) whole black peppercorns
- 20-40 g (0.7-1½ oz) ground Calabrian chilli, sweet or hot
- 0.5 g starter culture for cured sausages, or the dose on the pack
- 2 m (7 ft) natural hog casing (bung or middle), 50-60 mm (2-2¼ in) wide
- butcher's twine

**Extra equipment:** two clean wooden boards and a weight of 3-4 kg (6.6-8.8 lb).

## Method

1. Put the meat and fat in the freezer for 30-40 minutes, until firm at the edges but not frozen.
2. Cut the fat by hand into half-centimetre dice. Put it back in the fridge.
3. Cut the meat into 3 cm (1¼ in) cubes and grind it with the coarse 10-12 mm (⅜-½ in) plate. Or chop it by hand with a knife into 1 cm (⅜ in) pieces.
4. Weigh the salt, curing salt, pepper and chilli. Dissolve the culture in a tablespoon of cold, unchlorinated water.
5. Combine meat, fat, salts, spices and culture. Knead with your hands for 4-5 minutes, until the mixture is bound and sticky.
6. Fry a teaspoon of the mixture and taste it. Adjust only the chilli, never the curing salt.
7. Soak the casing in lukewarm water for 30 minutes and cut it into lengths of 25-30 cm (9¾-12 in). Tie one end of each piece with twine.
8. Stuff tightly, with no air pockets. Each soppressata should be 15-20 cm (6-7¾ in) long and firm to the touch.
9. Tie the other end with twine, pulling hard. Prick any remaining air bubbles with a needle.
10. Weigh each soppressata and write the weight on a tag.
11. Hang the soppressate for 48 hours at 20-24 °C (68-75 °F) with humidity above 90%.
12. Measure the pH: it must be 5.3 or lower. If it has not dropped within 72 hours, throw them away.
13. Lay the soppressate in a single layer between the two boards, put the weight on top and leave them in a cool place for 24 hours. They should flatten a little without splitting.
14. Tie them again in a net of string and hang them at 12-15 °C (54-59 °F) with 75-85% humidity.
15. Weigh them every week. They are ready when they have lost 30-35% of their weight, usually after 7-10 weeks. The PDO specification requires at least 45 days.
16. Cut the first soppressata. The slice should be compact and bright red, with the cubes of fat firmly attached to the meat.

## Tips
- Hand-cut fat stays in neat cubes and gives the slice its typical pattern.
- Whole peppercorns are traditional: grind them only if you want a stronger taste.

## Common mistakes
- Don't press too hard: the casing splits and air gets into the meat.
- Don't cure in a space that is too dry: a crust forms and stops the inside from drying.

## Local variations
- It is made **sweet** (sweet chilli) and **hot**: the hot one is darker.

## Storage
Whole: hung in a cool place or in the fridge, wrapped in paper, for 3-4 months. It can also be vacuum-packed. Once cut, cover the cut end with cling film and eat within 2-3 weeks.

## Substitutions
Hog bung casings are sold by butchers and online sausage-making shops. If you can't find ground Calabrian chilli, mix sweet (unsmoked) paprika and cayenne pepper.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
None in the basic ingredients.

## PDO and PGI
**Soppressata di Calabria PDO** is a certified product. Home-made soppressata cannot use this name.

## Wine
A medium-bodied red from the Cosenza area, such as a **Terre di Cosenza DOC red**.

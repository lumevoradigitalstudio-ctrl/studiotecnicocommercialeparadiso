# Easter lamb with peas

*Agnello con piselli di Pasqua · all of Calabria · Easter*

At Easter, in many southern Italian homes, the main course is lamb, and in spring fresh peas have just arrived in the garden. This recipe cooks them together: pieces of lamb browned in olive oil with onion, splashed with white wine and left to cook gently, then the peas, which take on the flavour of the meat juices. It is a simple, fragrant dish, made while the cuzzupe (recipe 57) bake in the oven. In some families, at the end, eggs beaten with pecorino are poured over it and set into a creamy coating `[TO BE VERIFIED: how common in Calabria]`. Here it is given as a variation. With frozen peas it works all year.

**Serves:** 6 · **Prep:** 20 minutes · **Cook:** 1 hour 30 minutes · **Total:** 1 hour 50 minutes · **Difficulty:** easy

## Ingredients

- 1.5 kg (3.3 lb) lamb (shoulder and chops), in pieces
- 5 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 1 garlic clove
- 150 ml (¾ cup) dry white wine
- 600 g (1.3 lb) shelled fresh peas, or frozen peas
- 1 sprig rosemary
- 200 ml (¾ cup) hot water or stock
- salt and pepper
- 1 handful chopped parsley

**For the egg variation**
- 3 eggs, 40 g (1½ oz) grated pecorino

## Method

1. Pat the pieces of lamb dry with paper towels.
2. Heat the oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat and brown the lamb for 10-12 minutes, a few pieces at a time, until golden on every side.
3. Add the onion and garlic, lower the heat and cook for 5 minutes, until the onion is soft.
4. Pour in the wine and let it evaporate for 2-3 minutes.
5. Add the rosemary, salt, pepper and the hot water.
6. Cover and cook over low heat for 50 minutes, turning now and then. Add a little water if the bottom dries out.
7. Add the peas and stir.
8. Cook for another 20 minutes with fresh peas (10-12 minutes with frozen), until they are tender and the meat is soft.
9. Check the salt and sprinkle with parsley.
10. **Egg variation:** beat the eggs with the pecorino. Take the pan off the heat, pour the eggs over the lamb and stir gently for 1 minute, until they set into a soft cream. Serve straight away.

## Tips
- The smallest fresh peas are the sweetest: add them in the last minutes.
- With milk-fed lamb, cooking is reduced to 40 minutes in all before adding the peas.

## Common mistakes
- Don't brown all the pieces together: they stew and don't brown.
- In the variation, don't pour in the eggs with the pan on the heat: they scramble.

## Local variations
- With potatoes instead of peas, in the oven.
- With kid instead of lamb (see also recipe 40).

## Storage
In the fridge for 2 days, without the egg variation. Reheat over low heat with a splash of water.

## Substitutions
Any lamb or kid works. Frozen peas are an excellent alternative.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
Sulphites (wine). Eggs and milk, if you make the egg and pecorino variation.

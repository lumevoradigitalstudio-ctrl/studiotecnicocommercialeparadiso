# Licurdia (onion and bread soup)

*Licurdia · Cosenza and Vibo Valentia · winter*

Licurdia is a soup of onions, stale bread and chilli, born in farmers' homes as a humble, warming dish. Sources disagree about where it comes from. Some link it to the city of Cosenza and its surroundings. Others to the area around Tropea, where the red onion that makes it sweet is grown. It is certainly Calabrian, and every family has its own version: with olive oil or with lard, with or without potatoes, with pecorino or caciocavallo. This recipe uses Tropea onions and potatoes. The toasted bread goes into the bowl and the boiling soup is poured over it.

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 20 minutes · **Cook:** 50 minutes · **Rest:** 5 minutes · **Total:** 1 hour 15 minutes · **Difficulty:** easy

## Ingredients

- 800 g (1.8 lb) Tropea red onions
- 300 g (11 oz) yellow-fleshed potatoes
- 4 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil, or 30 g (1 oz) lard (recipe 06)
- 1 dried chilli, or ½ teaspoon chilli flakes
- 1.2 l (5 cups) hot water or vegetable stock
- 8 slices stale durum wheat bread (recipe 23)
- 60 g (2 oz) grated pecorino or caciocavallo
- salt

## Method

1. Peel the onions and slice them thinly, 3 mm (⅛ in) thick.
2. Peel the potatoes and dice them into 1.5 cm (⅝ in) cubes.
3. Heat the oil in a heavy-based pot over low heat. Add the onions, chilli and a pinch of salt.
4. Cook the onions covered for 20 minutes, stirring now and then, until soft, translucent and lightly golden. If they stick, add a spoonful of water.
5. Add the potatoes and stir for 2 minutes.
6. Pour in the hot water and bring to the boil.
7. Cook over low heat for 25 minutes, until the potatoes are soft and starting to break up.
8. Check the salt.
9. Meanwhile toast the slices of bread in the oven at 200 °C (390 °F) for 5-6 minutes, or on a grill, until crisp.
10. Put 2 slices of bread in each soup bowl and sprinkle them with cheese.
11. Pour the boiling soup over them and leave for 5 minutes, so the bread soaks it up.
12. Serve with more cheese and a drizzle of raw olive oil.

## Tips
- Slow cooking of the onions is everything: they must turn sweet, not burnt.
- For a thicker soup, crush a few potatoes with the ladle.

## Common mistakes
- Don't slice the onions too thick: they stay crunchy.
- Don't use fresh bread: it falls apart in the soup straight away.

## Local variations
- With lard instead of olive oil.
- Without potatoes, just onions and bread.
- With a beaten egg stirred into the soup at the end `[TO BE VERIFIED]`.

## Storage
The soup without bread keeps in the fridge for 2 days. Add the bread when serving.

## Substitutions
If you can't find Tropea onions, use sweet red onions, or half red and half yellow onions.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
Gluten (bread), milk (cheese).

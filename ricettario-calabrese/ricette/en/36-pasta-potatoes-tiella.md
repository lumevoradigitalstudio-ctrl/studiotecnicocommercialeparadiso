# Pasta and potatoes alla tiella (one-pan baked pasta)

*Pasta e patate alla tiella · 'ara tieddra · all of Calabria · all year*

The *tieddra* is the baking dish, and this is what goes into it when the house has potatoes, a piece of cheese and a packet of pasta. Everything goes into the dish raw: pasta, chunks of potato, cherry tomatoes, onion, caciocavallo, pecorino, oregano, olive oil and water. Then it goes into the oven, with no frying first and no pans to wash. In the oven the pasta cooks in the water, the potatoes soften, the cheese melts and a golden crust forms on top. It seems impossible the first time, and then it becomes a house favourite. The secret is the amount of water, and a ten-minute rest before serving.

**Serves:** 6 · **Prep:** 25 minutes · **Cook:** 1 hour · **Rest:** 10 minutes · **Total:** 1 hour 35 minutes · **Difficulty:** easy

## Ingredients

- 400 g (14 oz) short, sturdy pasta (broken ziti, mezze maniche or pennoni)
- 800 g (1.8 lb) yellow-fleshed potatoes
- 300 g (11 oz) cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced
- 200 g (7 oz) semi-aged caciocavallo, diced
- 60 g (2 oz) grated pecorino
- 2 tablespoons dried breadcrumbs
- 1 tablespoon dried oregano
- 1 handful chopped parsley
- 80 ml (¼ cup) extra virgin olive oil
- about 900 ml (3¾ cups) hot water
- 10 g (0.4 oz) fine salt, pepper

**Equipment:** baking dish at least 6 cm (2¼ in) deep, about 30 × 35 cm (12 × 14 in).

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C (390 °F), conventional (no fan).
2. Peel the potatoes, wash them and cut them into 2-3 cm (¾-1¼ in) chunks.
3. Oil the dish with a tablespoon of the oil.
4. Put the raw pasta, potatoes, tomatoes, onion and half the caciocavallo in the dish, mixed together.
5. Season with salt, pepper, oregano, parsley, half the pecorino and the rest of the oil. Mix with your hands, so all the pasta is coated in oil.
6. Spread everything out evenly and pour the hot water in from the side. The water should come just below the surface: the pasta on top stays out of it.
7. Cover with foil, sealing the edges.
8. Bake for 35 minutes.
9. Remove the foil and taste a piece of pasta and a potato. If they are still hard and the water has gone, add 100 ml (½ cup) of hot water.
10. Scatter the rest of the caciocavallo, the pecorino and the breadcrumbs over the top.
11. Bake uncovered for another 20-25 minutes, until the top is golden and crisp and the water has been absorbed.
12. Leave it to rest for 10 minutes out of the oven before serving.

## Tips
- Use a large, thick pasta shape: thin ones fall apart in the oven.
- The pasta on top, out of the water, turns crunchy: it is the most fought-over part.

## Common mistakes
- Don't add too much water: you'll get a soup. Better to add more halfway through.
- Don't cut the potatoes too small: they break up.

## Local variations
- With pieces of sausage or pancetta, for a richer version.
- With a spoonful of 'nduja melted in the water.

## Storage
In the fridge, covered, for 2 days. Reheat in the oven at 180 °C (355 °F) with a splash of water.

## Substitutions
Instead of caciocavallo, provola or scamorza.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
Gluten (pasta, breadcrumbs), milk (cheeses).

## PDO and PGI
Optional: **Patata della Sila PGI**, **Caciocavallo Silano PDO**, **Cipolla Rossa di Tropea Calabria PGI**.

## Wine
A **Terre di Cosenza DOC red**.

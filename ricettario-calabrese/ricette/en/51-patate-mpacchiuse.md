# Patate 'mpacchiuse (sticky pan-fried potatoes)

*Patate 'mpacchiuse · patati 'mpacchiusi · Sila, province of Cosenza · autumn-winter*

*'Mpacchiuse* means sticky, stuck together. This side dish from the Sila is made with thinly sliced potatoes, cooked in a pan with a little oil and a little onion. There is only one rule: don't stir. The potatoes are left to stick to each other and to the pan, the pan is shaken and they are turned in blocks with a spatula. At the end they are soft inside, golden and crisp outside, joined together like a cake. They look fried, but there is little oil. The right potatoes are those from the Sila, which have had a PGI since 2010 and are rich in starch, because it is the starch that makes them stick.

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 15 minutes · **Cook:** 35 minutes · **Total:** 50 minutes · **Difficulty:** easy

## Ingredients

- 1 kg (2.2 lb) yellow-fleshed potatoes (Patata della Sila PGI)
- 4 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- ½ small red onion, thinly sliced
- salt

**Equipment:** a 28 cm (11 in) iron or non-stick frying pan with a lid; a wide spatula.

## Method

1. Peel the potatoes and slice them thinly, 3-4 mm (⅛ in) thick.
2. Don't wash them after slicing: the starch makes them stick. Just pat them dry with a tea towel.
3. Heat the oil in the pan over medium heat.
4. Add the potatoes and spread them out evenly. Salt them.
5. Cover and cook for 10 minutes over medium heat, without stirring. Shake the pan now and then.
6. Remove the lid. With the spatula, turn the potatoes over in blocks, without separating them: they should be golden underneath.
7. Add the onion among the potatoes.
8. Cook uncovered for another 20-25 minutes, turning in blocks every 5-6 minutes, until golden and crisp outside and soft inside.
9. Check the salt and serve hot.

## Tips
- Floury mountain potatoes stick together better than new potatoes.
- If the potatoes stick too much, add a spoonful of oil around the edge.

## Common mistakes
- Don't stir with a spoon: the potatoes break and lose their crust.
- Don't use too much oil: you'll get fried potatoes, not 'mpacchiuse.

## Local variations
- Without onion, just potatoes, oil and salt.
- With a pinch of chilli or with pieces of sausage.

## Storage
Best freshly made. Leftovers can be reheated in a pan the next day.

## Substitutions
Any yellow-fleshed, floury frying potato works.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
None.

## PDO and PGI
**Patata della Sila PGI**. Optional: **Cipolla Rossa di Tropea Calabria PGI**.

## Wine
A **Terre di Cosenza DOC red**.

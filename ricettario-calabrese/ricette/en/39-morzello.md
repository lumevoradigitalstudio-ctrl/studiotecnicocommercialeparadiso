# Morzello alla catanzarese (spicy tripe stew)

*Morzello alla catanzarese · morzeddu · Catanzaro · all year*

Morzello is the dish of Catanzaro. It is a red, spicy stew of veal tripe and offal, cooked for a long time in tomato with garlic, bay, oregano and chilli. It isn't eaten from a plate but inside the pitta (recipe 24), cut into quarters and opened like a little book, with your hands. It is humble cooking, born to use the less prized parts of the animal, and today it is a symbol of the city. Every family makes it its own way. This version uses tripe, heart and lung. Ask the butcher for them, already cleaned and, if possible, pre-cooked.

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 40 minutes · **Cook:** 2 hours 30 minutes · **Total:** about 3 hours 10 minutes · **Difficulty:** medium

## Ingredients

- 700 g (1.5 lb) veal tripe, already cleaned (better if pre-cooked)
- 300 g (11 oz) veal offal (heart and lung), already cleaned
- 4 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed
- 2 dried hot chillies, crumbled, or 1 teaspoon ground chilli
- 100 ml (½ cup) red wine, optional
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 700 g (1.5 lb) tomato passata
- 3 bay leaves
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- salt
- 4 pitte catanzaresi (recipe 24), for serving

## Method

1. Wash the tripe and offal in cold water.
2. If the tripe isn't pre-cooked, boil it in salted water with a bay leaf for 1 hour. Drain.
3. Blanch the heart and lung in salted water for 10 minutes. Drain.
4. Cut the tripe and offal into strips or pieces of 2-3 cm (¾-1¼ in).
5. In a heavy-based pan heat the oil with the garlic and chilli over low heat for 2 minutes, until the garlic is golden.
6. Add the tripe and offal and brown over medium heat for 10 minutes, stirring.
7. If using it, pour in the wine and let it evaporate for 2-3 minutes.
8. Dissolve the tomato paste in a ladle of hot water and add it with the passata, the bay and half the oregano. Add salt.
9. Bring to the boil, lower the heat to the minimum and cook covered for 2 hours, stirring every 20 minutes. Add a little hot water if the sauce gets too dry.
10. The morzello is ready when the tripe is very tender and the sauce is thick, dark red and spicy.
11. Add the rest of the oregano in the last 5 minutes.
12. Cut each pitta into quarters and open each quarter like a little book. Fill it with the hot morzello and serve straight away.

## Tips
- Morzello is even better the next day. Reheat it gently with a splash of water.
- If offal puts you off, start with tripe only: the dish still works.

## Common mistakes
- Don't cook on high heat: the tripe turns rubbery.
- Don't drain off the sauce: it is the part that soaks the pitta.

## Local variations
- **Salt cod morzello**, linked to the Christmas season.
- Pork or fish morzello, depending on the area and the season.

## Storage
In the fridge for 3 days, in a closed container. In the freezer for 3 months.

## Substitutions
Pre-cooked tripe is sold at butcher's counters and in many supermarkets (honeycomb tripe works well). If you can't find pitta, use a ring of rustic bread or a soft roll cut into a pocket.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
Sulphites (wine, if used). Gluten (pitta).

## PDO and PGI
None.

## Wine
A full, warm red, such as a **Cirò Rosso DOC**.

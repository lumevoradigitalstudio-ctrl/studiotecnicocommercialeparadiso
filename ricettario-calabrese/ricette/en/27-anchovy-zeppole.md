# Anchovy zeppole (fried dough puffs)

*Zeppole con le alici · zippule, crispelli · Catanzaro and Vibo Valentia · Immaculate Conception and Christmas*

On the evenings before the Feast of the Immaculate Conception, and then at Christmas, many Calabrian homes fry zeppole. Around Catanzaro they are called *zippule*, elsewhere *crispelli* or *crispelle*. They are made with a leavened dough so soft it can't be rolled out: you take it with wet hands, wrap it around an anchovy fillet and let it drop into the hot oil. They come out puffed, golden and irregular, each one different from the next. They are the simpler, savoury cousin of cuddrurieddri (recipe 26): no shaping here, and an even softer dough. Some families also make them without any filling, just with salt.

**Makes:** about 25 zeppole · **Prep:** 20 minutes · **Cook:** 30 minutes · **Rest:** 2 hours · **Total:** about 3 hours · **Difficulty:** easy

## Ingredients

- 500 g (1.1 lb) Italian 00 flour, or plain/all-purpose flour
- 380 ml (1½ cups) lukewarm water, about 30 °C (86 °F)
- 6 g (0.2 oz) fresh yeast, or 2 g dried yeast
- 10 g (0.4 oz) fine salt
- 12-13 anchovy fillets in oil, or salted and rinsed, broken in two
- 1 l (4¼ cups) oil for frying (olive oil or high-oleic sunflower oil)
- a bowl of cold water for wetting your hands

## Method

1. Dissolve the yeast in 100 ml (½ cup) of the lukewarm water.
2. Put the flour in a large bowl, pour in the water with the yeast and stir with a spoon.
3. Add the rest of the water a little at a time, then the salt.
4. Work the dough in the bowl with your hand, slapping it against the sides, for 8-10 minutes. It should become smooth, very soft, elastic and sticky: more a thick batter than a dough.
5. Cover and leave to rise for 2 hours in a warm place, until doubled and full of bubbles.
6. Dry the anchovy fillets well on paper towels.
7. Heat the oil to 175-180 °C (345-355 °F) in a deep pot.
8. Wet your hands in the cold water. Take a piece of dough the size of a large walnut and spread it on your palm.
9. Put a piece of anchovy in the middle and close the dough around it, pinching with your fingers.
10. Drop the zeppola into the oil. Fry 4-5 at a time.
11. Turn them with a slotted spoon and cook for 3-4 minutes in all, until puffed and golden on every side.
12. Drain on paper towels and serve hot.

## Tips
- Wet your hands before each zeppola: the dough won't stick.
- If the zeppole stay flat in the oil, the dough hasn't risen enough: wait another 30 minutes.

## Common mistakes
- Don't leave the anchovies wet: the oil spits.
- Don't fry over low heat: the zeppole soak up oil and stay heavy.

## Local variations
- With boiled potatoes in the dough, as for cuddrurieddri.
- With a piece of 'nduja or cheese instead of the anchovy.
- Unfilled, salted straight after frying, or sweet, rolled in sugar.

## Storage
Best freshly made. Leftovers keep for 1 day and can be reheated in the oven at 180 °C (355 °F) for 5 minutes.

## Substitutions
Anchovies in oil are sold everywhere. If you use salted ones, rinse them and soak them for 10 minutes in cold water.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
Gluten (wheat flour), fish (anchovies).

## PDO and PGI
None.

## Wine
A white from the Ionian coast, such as a **Melissa DOC white**.

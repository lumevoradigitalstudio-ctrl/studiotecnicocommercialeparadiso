# Courgette (zucchini) flower fritters

*Pitticelle di fiori di zucca · all of Calabria · summer*

In summer courgette plants fill with yellow flowers, and the ones that won't become courgettes end up in the kitchen. Pitticelle are soft, flat fritters made with a batter of flour and eggs, into which the flowers are torn, together with pecorino and parsley. They are fried by the spoonful and eaten straight away, hot, as an antipasto or a snack. This recipe is the flower version, which lasts only a few weeks in summer. Pick the flowers in the morning, when they are fully open, and use them the same day.

**Serves:** 4 (about 16 fritters) · **Prep:** 20 minutes · **Cook:** 20 minutes · **Rest:** 30 minutes · **Total:** 1 hour 10 minutes · **Difficulty:** easy

## Ingredients

- 15-20 fresh courgette or squash flowers
- 150 g (5½ oz) Italian 00 flour, or plain/all-purpose flour
- 2 medium eggs
- 150 ml (¾ cup) cold water, or milk
- 40 g (1½ oz) grated pecorino
- 1 tablespoon chopped parsley
- 1 pinch bicarbonate of soda, or 3 g instant savoury baking powder, optional, for puffier fritters
- salt and pepper
- 500 ml (2 cups) oil for frying (olive oil or high-oleic sunflower oil)

## Method

1. Gently open the flowers and remove the pistil inside and the tough stem.
2. Rinse them under a thin stream of cold water, shake them and dry them on paper towels.
3. Cut them into strips 1 cm (⅜ in) wide.
4. In a bowl, beat the eggs with the water, salt and pepper.
5. Add the flour a little at a time, whisking, until the batter is smooth and thick like yoghurt.
6. Add the pecorino, parsley, bicarbonate if using, and the flowers. Stir.
7. Cover and leave to rest for 30 minutes in the fridge.
8. Heat the oil to 170 °C (340 °F) in a deep frying pan.
9. Drop the batter into the oil by the spoonful, flattening it a little with the back of the spoon. Fry 4-5 fritters at a time.
10. Cook them for 2 minutes per side, until puffed and golden.
11. Drain them on paper towels, salt lightly and serve straight away.

## Tips
- Pick or buy the flowers in the morning: they are open and easier to clean.
- A cold batter makes lighter fritters.

## Common mistakes
- Don't fry over too low a heat: the fritters soak up oil.
- Don't leave the flowers wet: they thin the batter.

## Local variations
- With grated courgettes, once the flowers are over.

## Storage
They are best freshly made. Leftovers keep in the fridge for 1 day and can be reheated in the oven at 180 °C (355 °F) for 5 minutes.

## Substitutions
Courgette flowers are sold in summer at markets and by anyone with a vegetable garden. Otherwise, use grated and squeezed courgettes.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
Gluten (flour), eggs, milk (pecorino; and milk in the batter, if used).

## PDO and PGI
None.

## Wine
A white from the Locride, such as a **Bivongi DOC white**.

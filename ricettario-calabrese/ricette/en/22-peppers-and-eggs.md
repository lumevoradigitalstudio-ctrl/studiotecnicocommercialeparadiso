# Peppers and eggs

*Peperoni e uova · pipi e ova · all of Calabria · summer*

This is one of the simplest dishes in Calabrian cooking, and one of the best loved. Peppers are fried in oil until sweet and soft, then eggs are cracked into the pan and stirred until they set, without drying out. A few minutes and a slice of bread are enough for a complete lunch. It is a close relative of peppers and potatoes (recipe 50): same pan, same oil, and the eggs at the end. Many families put it in a sandwich to take away. Sweet peppers are used, green or red, but some add a few hot chillies.

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 10 minutes · **Cook:** 25 minutes · **Total:** 35 minutes · **Difficulty:** easy

## Ingredients

- 600 g (1.3 lb) sweet peppers, red, yellow or long green frying peppers
- 1 small red onion, optional
- 80 ml (¼ cup) extra virgin olive oil
- 6 medium eggs
- 30 g (1 oz) grated pecorino, optional
- salt
- 1 fresh hot chilli, optional

## Method

1. Wash the peppers, remove the stem, seeds and white membranes. Cut them into strips 2 cm (¾ in) wide and dry them well.
2. If using the onion, cut it into thin wedges.
3. Heat the oil in a wide frying pan over medium heat.
4. Add the peppers and cook for 15-18 minutes, turning every 3-4 minutes, until soft with a few golden patches.
5. Add the onion and the sliced chilli, and cook for 5 more minutes.
6. Salt the peppers.
7. Lightly beat the eggs with a pinch of salt and the pecorino.
8. Lower the heat and pour the eggs over the peppers.
9. Stir gently with a spatula for 1-2 minutes, until the eggs are set but still soft and glossy.
10. Turn off the heat straight away and serve.

## Tips
- Turn off the heat a moment before the eggs look done: the heat of the pan finishes the cooking.
- If you prefer it "frittata style", don't stir, and flip it as in recipe 20.

## Common mistakes
- Don't add the eggs over high heat: they turn dry and grainy.
- Don't cover the peppers while they cook: they steam instead of frying.

## Local variations
- With potatoes fried together with the peppers (see recipe 50).
- With a ripe tomato, chopped, added before the eggs.

## Storage
Best freshly made. Leftovers keep in the fridge for 1 day and are good cold in a sandwich.

## Substitutions
Any thin-walled sweet peppers work, including Italian frying peppers.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
Eggs; milk (pecorino, if used).

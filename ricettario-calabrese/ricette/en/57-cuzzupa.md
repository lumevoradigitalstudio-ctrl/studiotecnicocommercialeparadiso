# Cuzzupa (Easter bread with whole eggs)

*Cuzzupa di Pasqua · cuzzupa, cuddhura · all of Calabria · Easter*

Cuzzupa is the sweet bread of Easter. It is made with a soft, lemon-scented dough, shaped into a ring, a plait, a heart or a basket, and it carries one or more whole eggs in their shells, held in place by crossed strips of dough. The egg is the symbol of rebirth. The name changes from village to village, and so do the shapes. Cuzzupe are eaten at breakfast for days after Easter. Allow about four hours: the dough needs time to rise slowly.

**Makes:** 2 large or 6 small cuzzupe · **Prep:** 40 minutes · **Cook:** 35 minutes · **Rest:** 2 hours 30 minutes-3 hours · **Total:** about 4 hours · **Difficulty:** medium

## Ingredients

- 500 g (1.1 lb) Italian 00 flour, or plain/all-purpose flour
- 120 g (4 oz) sugar
- 2 medium eggs, for the dough
- 100 ml (½ cup) lukewarm milk
- 80 g (3 oz) lard (recipe 06) or soft butter
- 12 g (0.4 oz) fresh yeast, or 4 g dried yeast
- grated zest of 1 unwaxed lemon
- 1 pinch salt
- 2 to 6 whole eggs in their shells, washed and dried, for decoration
- 1 egg yolk and 1 tablespoon milk, for brushing
- coloured sprinkles, optional

## Method

1. Dissolve the yeast in the lukewarm milk with a teaspoon of the sugar.
2. In a bowl mix the flour, sugar, lemon zest and salt.
3. Add the milk with the yeast and the 2 beaten eggs, and knead.
4. Add the lard a little at a time and knead for 10 minutes, until the dough is smooth, soft and elastic.
5. Cover and leave to rise for 1 hour 30 minutes-2 hours in a warm place, until doubled.
6. Keep back a piece of dough the size of an egg for the strips.
7. Divide the rest into 2 parts (or 6 for small cuzzupe).
8. For a ring: roll a rope 50 cm (20 in) long and close it into a ring. For a plait: roll 3 ropes, plait them and close into a ring.
9. Put the cuzzupe on a tray lined with baking paper.
10. Press the whole eggs into the dough, spaced apart. Hold each egg in place with two thin strips of dough crossed in an X.
11. Cover and leave to rise for 1 hour.
12. Heat the oven to 180 °C (355 °F), conventional (no fan).
13. Brush the cuzzupe with the yolk beaten with the milk, avoiding the eggs. Add the sprinkles.
14. Bake for 30-35 minutes, until golden and hollow-sounding when tapped underneath.
15. Let them cool on a rack.

## Tips
- Use fresh eggs and wash them well, because they bake in the bread in their shells.
- If the dough is too sticky, oil your hands instead of adding flour.

## Common mistakes
- Don't put eggs straight from the fridge into the dough: let them come to room temperature.
- Don't open the oven in the first 20 minutes: the dough collapses.

## Local variations
- With aniseed in the dough.
- The eggs baked in the cuzzupa are eaten hard-boiled, peeled.

## Storage
In a food bag, for 3-4 days. The eggs baked in the bread should be eaten within 2 days, or kept in the fridge.

## Substitutions
The ingredients are available everywhere. Instead of lard, butter.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
Gluten (flour), eggs, milk.

## PDO and PGI
None.

## Wine
At breakfast it goes with coffee. After a meal, a **Greco di Bianco DOC**.

# Pasta china (festive baked pasta)

*Pasta china · pasta chjina, pasta 'mbottita · all of Calabria · Sundays and feast days*

*Chjina* means full, and this pasta truly is. In layers in the baking dish come short pasta dressed with tomato sauce, little fried meatballs, hard-boiled eggs, slices of soppressata, caciocavallo and pecorino. In the oven it all comes together and a golden crust forms on top. It is the dish for feast days and important Sundays, the one prepared the day before and brought to the table whole, in its dish. Every part of Calabria makes it its own way, and in some villages it is called *pasta 'mbottita*. It is different from Carnival *sagna chjina*, which is made with lasagne sheets. This recipe serves eight.

**Serves:** 8 · **Prep:** 1 hour 15 minutes · **Cook:** 1 hour 30 minutes · **Rest:** 15 minutes · **Total:** about 3 hours · **Difficulty:** medium

## Ingredients

**For the sauce**
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1.5 l (6¼ cups) tomato sauce (recipe 07) or passata
- basil, salt

**For the meatballs**
- 300 g (11 oz) minced meat, half beef and half pork
- 50 g (2 oz) stale bread, soaked and squeezed
- 1 egg
- 30 g (1 oz) grated pecorino
- 1 tablespoon chopped parsley, salt, pepper
- oil for frying

**For the dish**
- 600 g (1.3 lb) short pasta (mezze maniche, rigatoni or broken ziti)
- 4 hard-boiled eggs, sliced
- 150 g (5½ oz) soppressata (recipe 02), thinly sliced
- 250 g (9 oz) semi-aged caciocavallo, diced
- 60 g (2 oz) grated pecorino

**Equipment:** baking dish of about 30 × 40 cm (12 × 16 in).

## Method

**The sauce**
1. Heat the oil in a pan and cook the onion over low heat for 5 minutes, until translucent.
2. Add the tomato sauce and a pinch of salt and cook for 30 minutes over low heat. At the end add the basil.

**The meatballs**
3. Mix the meat, bread, egg, pecorino, parsley, salt and pepper.
4. Shape meatballs the size of a large hazelnut: you will get about 50.
5. Fry them in oil at 170 °C (340 °F) for 3-4 minutes, until golden. Drain on paper towels.

**The dish**
6. Heat the oven to 190 °C (375 °F), conventional (no fan).
7. Cook the pasta in boiling salted water for half the time on the packet. Drain.
8. Dress it with two-thirds of the sauce.
9. Spread a few spoonfuls of sauce over the bottom of the dish.
10. Make a first layer with a third of the pasta.
11. Scatter over half the meatballs, half the eggs, half the soppressata, half the caciocavallo and a third of the pecorino.
12. Cover with another third of the pasta and repeat the filling layer.
13. Finish with the last of the pasta, the remaining sauce and the rest of the pecorino.
14. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes.
15. Remove the foil and bake for another 15-20 minutes, until the top is golden and crisp at the edges.
16. Leave it to rest for 15 minutes out of the oven before cutting: it firms up and portions better.

## Tips
- It can be prepared the day before up to step 13 and kept in the fridge. In that case add 10 minutes of baking.
- Pasta cooked halfway finishes cooking in the oven in the sauce: don't overcook it beforehand.

## Common mistakes
- Don't skimp on the sauce: the pasta absorbs it in the oven and turns dry.
- Don't cut it straight out of the oven: the melting filling slides out.

## Local variations
- With peas or artichokes between the layers, in spring.
- With crumbled sausage instead of meatballs.
- Carnival *sagna chjina* uses the same filling but with lasagne sheets.

## Storage
In the fridge, covered, for 3 days. Reheat in the oven at 180 °C (355 °F) for 15 minutes, in portions or whole. It can be frozen in portions.

## Substitutions
Instead of caciocavallo, mild provolone or scamorza. Instead of soppressata, a coarse-grained salami.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
Gluten (pasta, bread), eggs, milk (cheeses).

## PDO and PGI
Optional: **Caciocavallo Silano PDO**.

## Wine
A **Cirò Rosso DOC**.

# Vrasciole (long Sunday meatballs)

*Vrasciole · all of Calabria · Sundays*

Vrasciole are the Calabrian Sunday meatballs, but they aren't round: they are shaped long and slightly flattened, like small rolls. The mixture is the home one: minced meat, bread soaked and squeezed, eggs, pecorino with a little Parmesan, garlic and parsley. They are fried until golden and eaten just like that, hot, or finished in the Sunday sauce (recipe 30). They have become one of Calabria's best-loved recipes, and in many homes they are made precisely at the weekend. With pasta and Sunday ragù, they complete the festive meal.

**Serves:** 4 (about 16 vrasciole) · **Prep:** 30 minutes · **Cook:** 30 minutes · **Rest:** 10 minutes · **Total:** 1 hour 10 minutes · **Difficulty:** easy

## Ingredients

- 500 g (1.1 lb) minced meat, half beef and half pork
- 150 g (5½ oz) stale bread, crusts removed
- 2 medium eggs
- 50 g (2 oz) grated pecorino
- 30 g (1 oz) grated Parmesan
- 1 garlic clove, finely chopped
- 2 tablespoons chopped parsley
- salt and pepper
- dried breadcrumbs, if needed
- 500 ml (2 cups) oil for frying (olive oil or high-oleic sunflower oil)

## Method

1. Soak the bread in cold water for 3 minutes, then squeeze it well.
2. In a bowl mix the meat, crumbled bread, eggs, cheeses, garlic, parsley, salt and pepper.
3. Knead with your hands for 3-4 minutes, until everything is well combined.
4. The mixture should be soft but easy to shape. If it is too wet, add a spoonful of breadcrumbs.
5. Leave it to rest for 10 minutes in the fridge.
6. With wet hands take a piece of mixture the size of an egg and shape it into a long roll, 8-10 cm (3¼-4 in) long and 2-3 cm (¾-1¼ in) thick. Flatten it slightly.
7. Heat the oil to 170 °C (340 °F) in a deep frying pan.
8. Fry 4-5 vrasciole at a time for 4-5 minutes, turning them, until golden on every side and cooked in the middle.
9. Drain them on paper towels.
10. Serve them hot, or simmer them for 15 minutes in tomato sauce over low heat.

## Tips
- Well-squeezed bread makes the vrasciole soft without letting them fall apart.
- Cut the first one in half: it must not be pink inside.

## Common mistakes
- Don't make them too thick: they burn outside before they are cooked inside.
- Don't turn them too often in the pan: they break.

## Local variations
- With a piece of caciocavallo or soppressata in the middle.
- Baked, at 200 °C (390 °F) for 25 minutes, turning them halfway.

## Storage
In the fridge for 2 days, also in sauce. They can be frozen after frying.

## Substitutions
Instead of pecorino, any hard aged cheese works.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
Gluten (bread), eggs, milk (cheeses).

## PDO and PGI
Optional: **Pecorino Crotonese PDO**.

## Wine
A **Terre di Cosenza DOC red**.

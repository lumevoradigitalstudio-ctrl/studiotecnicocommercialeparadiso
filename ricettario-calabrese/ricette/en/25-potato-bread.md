# Potato bread

*Pane di patate · pani 'i patati · Sila, province of Cosenza · autumn-winter*

On the Sila plateau, potatoes grow above a thousand metres, and they are so rich in starch that they have had their own PGI since 2010. Added to bread dough, they make it soft and moist, with a thin crust and a crumb that stays tender for days. It is a mountain bread, simple and full of flavour. It is eaten with cured meats, with cheese, or just with olive oil. The potatoes are boiled in their skins and mashed while still warm, so they stay dry and don't make the dough wet. The rest is like the home bread, but with a little less water.

**Makes:** 1 loaf of about 1 kg (2.2 lb) · **Prep:** 40 minutes · **Cook:** 1 hour 10 minutes · **Rest:** 2 hours 30 minutes-3 hours · **Total:** about 4 hours 30 minutes · **Difficulty:** medium

## Ingredients

- 250 g (9 oz) yellow-fleshed potatoes (Patata della Sila PGI), weighed raw
- 350 g (12 oz) Italian type 0 flour, or strong white bread flour
- 150 g (5½ oz) semola rimacinata (re-milled durum wheat semolina)
- 230 ml (1 cup) lukewarm water, about 30 °C (86 °F), plus a few spoonfuls if needed
- 7 g (0.2 oz) fresh yeast, or 3 g dried yeast
- 12 g (0.4 oz) fine salt
- 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil

## Method

1. Wash the potatoes and put them in cold water with their skins on. From the boil, cook them for 25-30 minutes, until a fork slides in easily.
2. Peel them while still hot and mash them with a potato ricer. Let them cool until they no longer feel warm to the hand.
3. Dissolve the yeast in the lukewarm water.
4. In a large bowl mix the flour, semolina and mashed potatoes.
5. Pour in the water with the yeast a little at a time, kneading. Add a few spoonfuls of water only if the dough stays dry: the potatoes release moisture.
6. Add the salt and oil and knead on the work surface for 10 minutes, until the dough is smooth and soft.
7. Cover and leave to rise for 1 hour 30 minutes-2 hours in a warm place, until doubled.
8. Shape a round loaf, tightening the surface as for the home bread (recipe 23).
9. Put it on the tray lined with baking paper, dust with semolina, cover and let it rise for 1 hour.
10. Heat the oven to 220 °C (430 °F), with a small pan of water on the bottom.
11. Cut a cross on the top and put it in the oven.
12. Bake for 15 minutes, remove the pan of water, lower to 200 °C (390 °F) and bake for another 25 minutes.
13. The bread is done when golden and hollow-sounding when tapped underneath.
14. Let it cool on a rack for at least 1 hour.

## Tips
- Floury, older potatoes work better than new potatoes, which are watery.
- Potato dough is stickier: oil your hands instead of adding flour.

## Common mistakes
- Don't use hot potatoes: they kill the yeast.
- Don't boil the potatoes peeled: they soak up water and the bread turns gummy.

## Storage
In a paper or cloth bag for 4-5 days: it stays soft longer than ordinary bread. It can be frozen in slices.

## Substitutions
Any yellow-fleshed, slightly floury potato works, such as Maris Piper or Yukon Gold.

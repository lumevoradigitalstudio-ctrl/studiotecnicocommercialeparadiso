# Alici arriganate (oregano-baked anchovies)

*Alici arriganate · the Calabrian coasts · spring-summer*

*Arriganate* means "with oregano", and in this dish oregano is almost everything. Fresh anchovies, cleaned and opened flat, are layered in a baking dish and covered with breadcrumbs, garlic, oregano, parsley and olive oil, then baked. They come out golden on top and soft underneath, with the scent of dried oregano. Sometimes cherry tomatoes or a little pecorino go into the same dish. The longest job is cleaning the anchovies: it is done with your fingers, in a few minutes, and becomes easy after the first few.

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 30 minutes · **Cook:** 20 minutes · **Total:** 50 minutes · **Difficulty:** easy

## Ingredients

- 800 g (1.8 lb) fresh anchovies
- 60 g (2 oz) dried breadcrumbs
- 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 2 teaspoons dried oregano
- 1 handful chopped parsley
- 20 g (0.7 oz) grated pecorino, optional
- 1 lemon (the juice, optional)
- 80 ml (¼ cup) extra virgin olive oil
- salt and chilli

## Method

1. Clean the anchovies: pull the head off downwards, so the innards come away too.
2. Open the anchovies flat with your thumb along the belly and remove the backbone, pulling it from the head end.
3. Rinse them quickly in cold water and dry them on paper towels.
4. Heat the oven to 200 °C (390 °F), conventional (no fan).
5. Mix the breadcrumbs, garlic, oregano, parsley, pecorino and a pinch of salt and chilli. Add 2 tablespoons of the oil and mix with your fingers.
6. Oil a baking dish with a tablespoon of the oil.
7. Lay a layer of opened anchovies, skin side down, side by side.
8. Sprinkle with half the seasoned breadcrumbs.
9. Make a second layer of anchovies and cover with the rest of the breadcrumbs.
10. Drizzle with the remaining oil and, if you like, the lemon juice.
11. Bake for 15-20 minutes, until the top is golden and crisp.
12. Serve hot or warm.

## Tips
- Fresh anchovies have bright eyes and shiny skin, and are stiff.
- If you'd rather not clean them, ask the fishmonger to open them flat.

## Common mistakes
- Don't leave the anchovies in water: they break and lose flavour.
- Don't overbake them: they dry out.

## Local variations
- With chopped cherry tomatoes between the layers.
- With capers and olives in the breadcrumbs.

## Storage
In the fridge for 1 day. They are good cold too.

## Substitutions
Fresh sardines, prepared the same way, work well.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
Fish. Gluten (breadcrumbs). Milk (pecorino, if used).

## PDO and PGI
Optional: a **Calabrian PDO** extra virgin olive oil, such as **Alto Crotonese PDO**.

## Wine
A **Melissa DOC white**.

# Ciambotta (summer vegetable stew)

*Ciambotta · all of Calabria · summer*

Ciambotta is the stew of the summer vegetable garden. Aubergines, peppers, potatoes, courgettes, onion and tomato cook together in a pan, slowly, until every vegetable is soft and the sauce is thick. It is eaten hot or warm, as a side dish, as a one-pot meal with bread, or with an egg cracked on top at the end. The name and the vegetables change from village to village, and similar dishes exist all over southern Italy. The secret is to add the vegetables one at a time, starting with the ones that take longest, so that at the end none is mushy and none is raw. It is even better the next day.

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 20 minutes · **Cook:** 45 minutes · **Total:** 1 hour 5 minutes · **Difficulty:** easy

## Ingredients

- 2 medium potatoes, about 300 g (11 oz)
- 1 large aubergine, about 400 g (14 oz)
- 2 peppers, about 400 g (14 oz)
- 2 courgettes, about 300 g (11 oz)
- 1 red onion
- 400 g (14 oz) ripe or tinned plum tomatoes
- 6 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 1 chilli, optional
- 6-8 basil leaves
- salt

## Method

1. Wash all the vegetables. Peel the potatoes.
2. Cut the potatoes, aubergine and courgettes into 2 cm (¾ in) cubes, the peppers into 3 cm (1¼ in) pieces and the onion into slices.
3. Heat the oil in a wide pan over medium heat and cook the onion for 5 minutes, until soft.
4. Add the potatoes and cook for 10 minutes, stirring, until they start to brown.
5. Add the peppers and cook for 5 minutes.
6. Add the aubergine and cook for 5 minutes, stirring.
7. Add the courgettes, the chopped tomatoes, the chilli and a pinch of salt.
8. Cover and cook for 20 minutes over low heat, stirring gently now and then. Add a little water if it sticks.
9. Uncover and cook for another 5 minutes, until the sauce thickens.
10. Check the salt, add the torn basil and serve.

## Tips
- If you have time, fry the aubergines separately and add them at the end: the ciambotta will be richer.
- Use a wide pan: crowded vegetables release too much water.

## Common mistakes
- Don't put everything in at the start: the courgettes fall apart and the potatoes stay hard.
- Don't stir hard: the vegetables break.

## Local variations
- With eggs: at the end of cooking make 4 hollows in the ciambotta, crack an egg into each, cover and cook for 5 minutes.
- Without potatoes, just summer vegetables.

## Storage
In the fridge for 3 days. It is good cold too.

## Substitutions
Any summer vegetables work. Use the freshest you can find.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
None (eggs, if you make the variation).

# Goat braised with wine and tomato

*Capra in umido · Reggio Calabria, Aspromonte · summer festivals*

In the Calabrian mountains, and above all in the Aspromonte, goat has always been the meat of shepherds and of feast days. It is a tasty, rather strong meat that needs long, patient cooking. For feast days it is cooked in large pots, and the sauce dresses the maccheroni (recipe 30) before the meat comes to the table. This recipe is a home version: the pieces of goat are browned while splashed with vinegar, then cooked for hours with wine, onion and tomato, over very low heat. The result is meat that falls off the bone and a thick, dark sauce.

**Serves:** 6 · **Prep:** 30 minutes · **Cook:** 3 hours 30 minutes-4 hours · **Rest:** 4 hours (optional) · **Difficulty:** medium

## Ingredients

- 2 kg (4.4 lb) goat or kid on the bone, in pieces (ask the butcher)
- 100 ml (½ cup) red wine vinegar
- 5 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 2 onions, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves
- 300 ml (1¼ cups) red wine
- 1.5 l (6¼ cups) tomato passata
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 sprig rosemary
- 1-2 dried chillies
- salt and pepper

**For the marinade (optional)**
- 500 ml (2 cups) red wine, 2 bay leaves, 1 garlic clove

## Method

1. Wash the pieces of meat in cold water and trim off excess fat: that is what gives the strongest smell.
2. For a milder flavour, marinate the meat in the fridge for 4 hours with the wine, bay and garlic. Then drain and dry it well. Discard the marinade.
3. Heat the oil in a large heavy-based pan over medium-high heat.
4. Brown the goat for 15 minutes, a few pieces at a time, turning them, until golden.
5. While browning, splash the meat 3-4 times with a little vinegar, letting it evaporate each time.
6. Add the onion and garlic and cook for 5 minutes, stirring.
7. Pour in the wine and let it evaporate for 3-4 minutes.
8. Add the passata, bay, rosemary, chilli and a pinch of salt. Bring to the boil.
9. Lower the heat to the minimum, cover and cook for 3½-4 hours, stirring every 30 minutes. Add hot water if the sauce gets too dry.
10. The goat is ready when the meat comes off the bone with a fork.
11. Check the salt and pepper.
12. Use some of the sauce to dress pasta and serve the meat as the main course.

## Tips
- Kid is more tender and delicate than adult goat: it cooks in about 2 hours.
- Like all braises, it is better the next day.

## Common mistakes
- Don't rush it: on high heat goat meat stays tough.
- Don't skip the vinegar when browning: it removes some of the strong smell.

## Local variations
- **Boiled goat:** the meat is blanched and then simmered for a long time in a broth of vegetables, herbs and wine. The broth is used for pasta.
- **Easter kid**, in tomato sauce or plain, without tomato.

## Storage
In the fridge for 3 days, in its sauce. In the freezer for 3 months.

## Substitutions
Goat and kid are sold by halal butchers and at market butchers. Otherwise use lamb: it cooks in 2 hours.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
Sulphites (wine, vinegar).

## PDO and PGI
None.

## Wine
A **Cirò Rosso DOC**.

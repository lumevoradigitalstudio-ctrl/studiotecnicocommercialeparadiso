# Salt cod with potatoes and olives

*Baccalà con patate e olive · Cosenza, common across the region · Christmas Eve*

Baccalà, salted cod that came from far away and kept for a long time, was for many years the inexpensive fish of meatless days. On Christmas Eve in Calabria it is almost never missing. Around Cosenza it is cooked with Sila potatoes, black olives, tomato and parsley, in a pan where everything takes on flavour together. The cod must be soaked for two or three days, or bought already soaked from the fishmonger. After that, you only need to put everything in the pan and wait.

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 20 minutes · **Cook:** 50 minutes · **Total:** 1 hour 10 minutes (with pre-soaked cod) · **Difficulty:** easy

## Ingredients

- 800 g (1.8 lb) pre-soaked salt cod, or 500 g (1.1 lb) salted cod
- 700 g (1.5 lb) yellow-fleshed potatoes
- 6 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 1 onion, thinly sliced
- 1 garlic clove
- 400 g (14 oz) tinned plum tomatoes or cherry tomatoes
- 100 g (3½ oz) black olives
- 1 tablespoon desalted capers, optional
- 1 chilli, optional
- 1 handful chopped parsley
- salt, if needed, and pepper

## Method

**If the cod is salted**
1. Rinse it under running water to remove the salt on the surface.
2. Soak it in cold water in the fridge for 48-72 hours, changing the water 3 times a day. Taste a small piece: it should be only slightly salty.

**The dish**
3. Remove the skin and bones from the cod and cut it into 5-6 cm (2-2¼ in) pieces. Dry it well.
4. Peel the potatoes and slice them 1 cm (⅜ in) thick.
5. In a wide pan heat the oil and cook the onion and garlic over low heat for 5 minutes, until the onion is soft.
6. Add the crushed tomatoes and the chilli and cook for 5 minutes.
7. Add the potatoes and 200 ml (¾ cup) of hot water. Cover and cook for 20 minutes over low heat, until the potatoes are almost tender.
8. Lay the pieces of cod on top of the potatoes, skin side down if you have left it on.
9. Add the olives and capers.
10. Cover and cook for 15 minutes over low heat, without stirring. Shake the pan now and then so it doesn't stick.
11. The cod is ready when it is white and flakes apart.
12. Taste the sauce, add salt only if needed, a grind of pepper and the parsley. Serve hot.

## Tips
- Pre-soaked cod from the fishmonger saves time: ask for it desalted "ready to cook".
- A soft slice of potato crushed into the sauce thickens it.

## Common mistakes
- Don't stir the cod: it falls apart.
- Don't salt before tasting: the cod may still be salty.

## Local variations
- **Fried salt cod** with peppers and potatoes (see recipe 50).
- **Salt cod salad**, boiled and dressed with oil, olives, pickled peppers and garlic.
- Salt cod morzello, in Catanzaro (see recipe 39).

## Storage
In the fridge for 2 days. Reheat over low heat.

## Substitutions
Baccalà is salted cod; stockfish (recipe 46) is cod dried without salt. They are not the same thing, but both work in this dish, with different soaking times.

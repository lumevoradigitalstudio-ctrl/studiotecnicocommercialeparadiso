# Swordfish alla bagnarota

*Pesce spada alla bagnarota · Reggio Calabria, Bagnara Calabra · May-July*

Bagnara Calabra, on the coast of the Strait of Messina, is the swordfish town. From late spring into summer the boats go out to fish for it in the Strait. *Alla bagnarota* means cooked in a pan with a light sauce of cherry tomatoes, red onion, olives, capers, chilli and oregano, splashed with white wine. The fish cooks for just a few minutes and stays tender and juicy. It is a quick dish, ready in half an hour. The secret is to buy thick steaks and not to overcook them: overcooked swordfish turns dry and stringy.

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 15 minutes · **Cook:** 20 minutes · **Total:** 35 minutes · **Difficulty:** easy

## Ingredients

- 4 swordfish steaks, 2 cm (¾ in) thick, about 180 g (6½ oz) each
- 4 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil, plus a drizzle for serving
- 1 small red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 garlic clove, sliced
- 300 g (11 oz) cherry tomatoes, halved
- 20 black or green olives, pitted
- 1 heaped tablespoon desalted capers
- 1 fresh or dried chilli
- 100 ml (½ cup) dry white wine
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 handful chopped parsley
- salt

## Method

1. Rinse the steaks and pat them dry with paper towels.
2. In a wide frying pan heat the oil over medium heat and add the onion, garlic and chilli. Cook for 4-5 minutes, until the onion is soft.
3. Add the tomatoes, olives and capers. Cook for 5 minutes, until the tomatoes start to soften.
4. Push the sauce to the sides of the pan and put the steaks in the middle.
5. Cook them for 2 minutes per side over medium-high heat.
6. Pour in the wine and let it evaporate for 1-2 minutes.
7. Spoon the sauce over the fish, add a little salt and sprinkle with oregano.
8. Cover the pan and cook for 4-5 minutes over low heat. The fish is ready when it is white and opaque in the centre and just flakes with a fork.
9. Sprinkle with parsley, add a drizzle of raw olive oil and serve straight away.

## Tips
- Buy steaks at least 2 cm (¾ in) thick: thin ones dry out in a moment.
- Fresh swordfish is pale pink and glossy, with no dark patches.

## Common mistakes
- Don't overcook the fish: it turns hard and dry.
- Don't over-salt: olives and capers are already salty.

## Local variations
- Without tomato, with just oil, oregano, capers and a few breadcrumbs, in the oven.
- With Calabrian olives of the Ottobratica variety.

## Storage
Best freshly made. Leftovers keep in the fridge for 1 day: flake them and use them to dress pasta.

## Substitutions
Instead of swordfish, tuna or amberjack steaks. Frozen swordfish should be thawed in the fridge and dried well.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
Fish. Sulphites (wine).

## PDO and PGI
Optional: **Cipolla Rossa di Tropea Calabria PGI**.

## Wine
A white from the coast of Reggio, such as a **Bivongi DOC white**.

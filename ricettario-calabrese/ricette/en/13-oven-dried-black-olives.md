# Oven-dried black olives

*Olive nere al forno · all of Calabria · November-December*

Ripe, glossy black olives picked in late autumn don't need brine. They are buried in coarse salt for a few weeks, until they lose their bitterness and turn wrinkled, and then they go into the oven. They come out dry, soft and concentrated, with a taste that recalls dried fruit. They are dressed just before serving with a drizzle of oil, chilli, fennel or orange zest, and eaten with bread, with cheese or tossed through pasta. Every household has its own oven time and temperature. This recipe gives you a starting point and the signs that tell you they are ready.

**Makes:** about 800 g (1.8 lb) · **Prep:** 30 minutes · **Cook:** 45-60 minutes · **Rest:** 2-3 weeks · **Difficulty:** easy

## Ingredients

- 1 kg (2.2 lb) ripe black olives, firm and unblemished
- 100 g (3½ oz) coarse salt
- to dress when serving: extra virgin olive oil, dried chilli flakes, fennel seeds, grated orange zest, dried oregano

**Equipment:** a large colander or wicker basket, a plate to cover, a baking tray with baking paper.

## Method

1. Wash the olives in cold water and discard any that are soft or pierced.
2. Dry them well with a tea towel.
3. Mix them with the coarse salt in the colander, set over a bowl to catch the liquid.
4. Cover with a plate and leave in a cool place.
5. Stir the olives with your hands every day. Throw away the dark liquid that collects in the bowl.
6. After 2 weeks taste an olive (rinse it first). They are ready when they are wrinkled and only slightly bitter, usually in 2-3 weeks `[TO BE VERIFIED: traditional time]`.
7. Rinse the olives quickly under running water to remove excess salt and dry them carefully.
8. Heat the oven to 150 °C (300 °F), conventional (no fan).
9. Spread the olives in a single layer on the tray lined with baking paper.
10. Bake for 45-60 minutes, turning them every 15 minutes. They are ready when the skin is matt and a little wrinkled and the flesh is soft, but not dry.
11. Let them cool on the tray.
12. When serving, dress them with oil, chilli and the flavourings you like.

## Tips
- Larger olives need a few more minutes: check them from minute 40.
- Dress only what you will eat: plain olives keep better.

## Common mistakes
- Don't bake at too high a temperature: the skin burns and the flesh turns bitter.
- Don't store them already dressed with fresh garlic outside the fridge.

## Local variations
- Some families bake them in the wood-fired oven as it cools after the bread `[TO BE VERIFIED]`.
- In other homes they are dressed with sliced garlic and bay and eaten straight away.

## Storage
- Plain (undressed): in a closed glass jar, cool and dark, for about 1 month.
- Dressed: in the fridge, within 1 week.

## Substitutions
Fresh ripe black olives are found in autumn at markets. Don't use olives already in brine.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
None.

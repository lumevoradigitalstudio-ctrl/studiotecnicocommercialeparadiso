# Pasta with sun-dried tomatoes, olives and breadcrumbs

*Pasta con pomodori secchi, olive e mollica · all of Calabria · all year*

This is the pantry pasta, the one you make in twenty minutes when the fridge is empty but the shelves are not. Sun-dried tomatoes in oil (recipe 08), oven-dried black olives (recipe 13), a little chilli and toasted breadcrumbs are enough for a full-flavoured dish. It isn't an old recipe and it has no dialect name: it simply brings together the preserves in this book in the simplest way. The oil from the sun-dried tomatoes is already a dressing: use it. Instead of cheese there are crunchy breadcrumbs, as in humble Calabrian cooking. It works with any long or short pasta.

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 10 minutes · **Cook:** 15 minutes · **Total:** 25 minutes · **Difficulty:** easy

## Ingredients

- 360 g (13 oz) spaghetti, linguine or short pasta
- 100 g (3½ oz) sun-dried tomatoes in oil, drained
- 3 tablespoons of the oil from the sun-dried tomatoes
- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 80 g (3 oz) oven-dried black olives, or other flavourful black olives, pitted
- 1 garlic clove, sliced
- 1 teaspoon dried chilli flakes
- 1 tablespoon desalted capers, optional
- 2 anchovy fillets, optional
- 60 g (2 oz) stale bread, crumbled
- 1 handful parsley or a few basil leaves

## Method

1. Bring 4 l (4¼ quarts) of water to the boil with 30 g (1 oz) of coarse salt.
2. Toast the breadcrumbs in a small pan with the extra virgin olive oil over medium heat for 4-5 minutes, stirring, until golden. Set aside.
3. Cut the sun-dried tomatoes into strips and the olives into pieces.
4. In a large pan heat the tomato oil with the garlic and chilli over low heat for 2 minutes, until the garlic is golden.
5. If using them, add the anchovies and melt them off the heat.
6. Add the tomatoes, olives and capers and cook for 2 minutes over low heat.
7. Cook the pasta for the time on the packet, minus one minute. Keep back a cup of cooking water.
8. Drain the pasta into the pan, add a splash of cooking water and toss for 1 minute over high heat.
9. Turn off the heat, add the parsley and half the breadcrumbs and mix.
10. Serve with the rest of the breadcrumbs on top.

## Tips
- Taste before salting the water: sun-dried tomatoes, olives and capers are already salty.
- Make extra breadcrumbs: they keep for a few days in a jar.

## Common mistakes
- Don't cook the sun-dried tomatoes for long: they turn hard.
- Don't add the breadcrumbs too early: they lose their crunch.

## Local variations
- With a handful of rocket or sautéed turnip tops.
- With a spoonful of home-made ricotta (recipe 19) on each plate.

## Storage
Best freshly made. Leftovers are good cold the next day.

## Substitutions
Sun-dried tomatoes in oil and black olives are sold everywhere. Choose naturally ripened black olives, not ones blackened in brine.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
Gluten (pasta, bread). Fish, if using anchovies.

## PDO and PGI
Optional: a **Calabrian PDO** extra virgin olive oil or **Olio di Calabria PGI**.

## Wine
A **Cirò Rosato DOC**.

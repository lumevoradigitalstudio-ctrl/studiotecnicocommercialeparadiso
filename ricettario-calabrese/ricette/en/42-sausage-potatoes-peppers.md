# Baked sausage with potatoes and peppers

*Salsiccia con patate e peperoni al forno · all of Calabria · all year*

One tray, a hot oven and almost nothing to wash up: this is the main course of working days. Chunks of sausage, potatoes, peppers and onion cook together. The fat from the sausage dresses the vegetables and the potatoes turn golden and crisp at the edges. It is a home dish that needs no history, made in every Calabrian family with sausages from the butcher or home-made ones (recipe 01). Hot sausage gives its flavour to the whole tray. With sweet sausage, add a chilli. It goes to the table in its tray, with bread to mop up the juices.

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 20 minutes · **Cook:** 50 minutes · **Total:** 1 hour 10 minutes · **Difficulty:** easy

## Ingredients

- 600 g (1.3 lb) fresh sausage, sweet or hot
- 800 g (1.8 lb) yellow-fleshed potatoes
- 2 red or yellow peppers, about 400 g (14 oz)
- 1 red onion
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 1 sprig rosemary or 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 fresh chilli, optional, with sweet sausage
- salt and pepper

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C (390 °F), fan, or 220 °C (430 °F) conventional.
2. Peel the potatoes and cut them into 2 cm (¾ in) wedges. Wash and dry them well.
3. Remove the seeds and membranes from the peppers and cut them into strips 3 cm (1¼ in) wide.
4. Cut the onion into wedges.
5. Cut the sausage into 5-6 cm (2-2¼ in) pieces and prick them with a fork.
6. In a large tray mix the potatoes, peppers and onion with the oil, rosemary, salt and pepper.
7. Spread the vegetables in a single layer and put the sausage on top.
8. Bake for 25 minutes.
9. Turn the vegetables and sausage with a spatula.
10. Bake for another 20-25 minutes, until the potatoes are golden and soft inside and the sausage is cooked through, with no pink in the middle (at least 71 °C (160 °F) at the centre).
11. Serve hot, with the juices from the tray.

## Tips
- Use a large tray: if the vegetables are crowded, they steam instead of browning.
- Pricking the sausage lets the fat run out and dress the potatoes.

## Common mistakes
- Don't cut the potatoes too big: they stay raw when everything else is ready.
- Don't over-salt: sausage is already well seasoned.

## Local variations
- With oven-dried black olives (recipe 13) for the last 10 minutes.
- With mushrooms instead of peppers, in autumn.

## Storage
In the fridge, covered, for 2 days. Reheat in the oven at 180 °C (355 °F) for 10 minutes.

## Substitutions
Any coarse-ground pork sausage works, such as Italian sausage. If it is sweet, add a chilli or a pinch of chilli powder.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
None in the basic ingredients. Check the label of shop-bought sausage: it sometimes contains milk, mustard or sulphites.

## PDO and PGI
Optional: **Patata della Sila PGI**, **Cipolla Rossa di Tropea Calabria PGI**. **Salsiccia di Calabria PDO** can be used if you buy it certified.

## Wine
A **Savuto DOC red**.

# Maccheroni al ferretto with Sunday ragù

*Maccheroni al ferretto con ragù della domenica · Cosenza, common across the region · Sundays and feast days*

On Sundays, in many Calabrian homes, the sauce starts cooking early in the morning. Pieces of pork and sausages brown in olive oil with onion, then are splashed with red wine and covered with tomato sauce. The ragù simmers gently for hours, and meanwhile the maccheroni are made: tubes of semolina dough rolled on a thin rod (*ferretto*), shorter and more regular than fileja (recipe 29). At midday the sauce dresses the pasta, and the meat follows as the main course. It is the meal of the family gathered together. It is made slowly and eaten slowly. This recipe serves six, and leftover ragù is even better the next day.

**Serves:** 6 · **Prep:** 1 hour 15 minutes · **Cook:** 3 hours · **Rest:** 30 minutes · **Total:** about 4 hours · **Difficulty:** medium

## Ingredients

**For the ragù**
- 4 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 600 g (1.3 lb) pork shoulder or spare ribs, in large pieces
- 400 g (14 oz) fresh sausage (recipe 01 or from the butcher)
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 150 ml (¾ cup) red wine
- 2 l (2 quarts) tomato sauce (recipe 07) or passata
- 1 dried chilli, optional
- 1 bunch basil
- salt

**For the maccheroni**
- 600 g (1.3 lb) semola rimacinata (re-milled durum wheat semolina)
- about 300 ml (1¼ cups) warm water, 35-40 °C (95-104 °F)
- 1 pinch salt

**To finish**
- 60 g (2 oz) grated pecorino or aged caciocavallo

**Equipment:** a maccheroni rod or thin, smooth metal rod 2-3 mm (⅛ in) thick and at least 30 cm (12 in) long; a large heavy-based pan.

## Method

**The ragù**
1. Heat the oil in the pan over medium heat. Brown the pork for 10 minutes, turning it, until golden on every side.
2. Add the whole sausages and brown them for 5 minutes.
3. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes, until soft.
4. Pour in the wine and let it evaporate for 2-3 minutes, until you no longer smell the alcohol.
5. Add the tomato sauce, the chilli and a pinch of salt. Bring to the boil.
6. Lower the heat to the minimum, cover leaving a gap and cook for 2½-3 hours. Stir every 20 minutes. The ragù is ready when it is thick and dark and the meat comes off the bone with a fork.
7. At the end of cooking add the basil and check the salt.

**The maccheroni**
8. While the ragù cooks, knead the semolina, warm water and salt for 10-12 minutes, until the dough is smooth and firm. Cover it and leave it to rest for 30 minutes.
9. Take a piece of dough and roll it into a rope 1 cm (⅜ in) thick. Cut it into pieces 6-7 cm (2¼-2¾ in) long.
10. Lay the rod lengthwise on a piece of dough and press lightly.
11. Roll the rod back and forth under your palms, until the dough wraps around it and forms a tube 10-12 cm (4-4¾ in) long.
12. Slide the maccherone off and lay it on a tray with semolina. Repeat with all the dough.

**Cooking**
13. Lift the meat out of the ragù and keep it warm on a plate with a few ladles of sauce.
14. Bring 5 l (5¼ quarts) of water to the boil with 40 g (1½ oz) of coarse salt. Cook the maccheroni for 5-7 minutes from when the water returns to the boil. Taste one: it should be tender but firm.
15. Drain and dress them with the ragù and the grated cheese.
16. Serve the pasta, then bring the meat to the table with its sauce.

## Tips
- The ragù can be made the day before: after resting it tastes even better.
- If the dough sticks to the rod, dust the rod with semolina every few maccheroni.

## Common mistakes
- Don't boil the ragù on high heat: it sticks to the bottom and tastes burnt.
- Don't overcook the maccheroni: fresh semolina pasta cooks in a few minutes.

## Local variations
- With goat or wild boar instead of pork, in the mountains.
- With stuffed pork rolls or vrasciole (recipe 43) cooked in the ragù.

## Storage
- Ragù: 3 days in the fridge, 3 months in the freezer.
- Raw maccheroni: in the fridge for 1 day, or in the freezer for 1 month (cook them from frozen, 2 minutes longer).

## Substitutions
**Rod:** a thin knitting needle or a smooth metal skewer works. **Sausage:** any coarse-ground pork sausage, with a pinch of chilli.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
Gluten (durum wheat semolina), milk (cheese), sulphites (wine). Celery, if you add a celery base to the sauce.

## PDO and PGI
Optional: **Caciocavallo Silano PDO** or **Pecorino Crotonese PDO** for grating.

## Wine
A **Savuto DOC red**.

# Pipi chini (peppers stuffed with breadcrumbs)

*Peperoni ripieni di mollica · pipi chini · all of Calabria · summer*

*Pipi chini* means full peppers. They are filled with a humble, tasty mixture: stale bread soaked and squeezed, grated cheese, capers, basil, garlic, sometimes anchovies and a little tomato sauce. Then they are baked, or fried in a pan in olive oil, the version many consider the most traditional. They come out soft, with slightly charred skins and a golden filling. They are eaten hot, warm or cold, even the next day. There is also a richer version with minced meat. This is the breadcrumb one, said to be the oldest `[TO BE VERIFIED]`.

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 30 minutes · **Cook:** 40 minutes · **Total:** 1 hour 10 minutes · **Difficulty:** easy

## Ingredients

- 4 medium red or yellow peppers, or 8 small round peppers
- 200 g (7 oz) stale bread, crusts removed
- 60 g (2 oz) grated pecorino or Parmesan
- 1 tablespoon desalted capers
- 3 anchovy fillets, optional
- 1 garlic clove, chopped
- 6-8 basil leaves
- 1 handful chopped parsley
- 4 tablespoons tomato sauce
- 6 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- salt and pepper

## Method

1. Wash the peppers. Cut off the top with the stem and keep it. Remove the seeds and white membranes.
2. Soak the bread in cold water for 3 minutes and squeeze it well.
3. Crumble it into a bowl with the cheese, capers, torn anchovies, garlic, basil, parsley, 2 tablespoons of tomato sauce, 2 tablespoons of oil, salt and pepper.
4. Mix with your hands: the filling should be soft and compact.
5. Heat the oven to 190 °C (375 °F), conventional (no fan).
6. Fill the peppers, pressing well, up to the rim.
7. Close them with their tops.
8. Stand them upright in a baking dish, side by side. Pour the rest of the sauce and oil over them, and 100 ml (½ cup) of water into the bottom of the dish.
9. Bake for 40 minutes, until the peppers are soft and the skin is slightly darkened.
10. Let them cool for 10 minutes before serving.

## Tips
- Small round peppers cook faster and are served whole: check them after 30 minutes.
- Stale durum wheat bread holds the filling together better.

## Common mistakes
- Don't leave the bread too wet: the filling turns to mush.
- Don't add too much salt: capers, anchovies and cheese are already salty.

## Local variations
- **Fried:** cook the stuffed peppers in a pan with 1 cm (⅜ in) of oil, covered, over medium-low heat for 25-30 minutes, turning now and then.
- **With meat:** a filling of minced beef or pork, pecorino, egg and breadcrumbs.

## Storage
In the fridge for 2 days. They are good cold too.

## Substitutions
Any fleshy sweet peppers work.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
Gluten (bread), milk (cheese). Fish, if using anchovies.

## PDO and PGI
None.

## Wine
A **Cirò Rosato DOC**.

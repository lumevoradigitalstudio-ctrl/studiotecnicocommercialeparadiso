# Millecosedde (mixed bean and pasta soup)

*Millecosedde · Cosenza · winter*

The name means something like "a thousand little things", and it says it all. Millecosedde is a thick soup of different legumes, cooked together with vegetables, mushrooms and pasta, and dressed with olive oil and pecorino. Everyone puts in what they have in the pantry: chickpeas, beans, lentils, dried broad beans, grass peas, a piece of Savoy cabbage, a handful of dried mushrooms. There is no right recipe, because the rule is precisely to waste nothing. It is the dish for winter evenings in the mountains, warming and filling. This version uses five legumes and dried porcini. Change it with whatever you have: it will still be good.

**Serves:** 6 · **Prep:** 30 minutes · **Cook:** 2 hours · **Rest:** 12-24 hours soaking · **Difficulty:** easy

## Ingredients

- 300 g (11 oz) mixed dried legumes: chickpeas, cannellini or borlotti beans, dried broad (fava) beans, grass peas (cicerchie)
- 80 g (3 oz) lentils (no soaking needed)
- 20 g (0.7 oz) dried porcini mushrooms
- 4 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil, plus a drizzle for serving
- 1 onion, 1 celery stick, 1 carrot, chopped
- 80 g (3 oz) diced pancetta, optional
- 300 g (11 oz) Savoy cabbage, in strips
- 2 tablespoons tomato sauce, optional
- 1 dried chilli, optional
- 200 g (7 oz) short pasta (ditalini, tubetti or mixed broken pasta)
- 60 g (2 oz) grated pecorino
- salt and pepper

## Method

1. The night before, soak the chickpeas, beans, broad beans and grass peas in plenty of cold water for 12-24 hours.
2. Drain them, discard the soaking water and rinse the legumes.
3. Put the legumes in a large pot with 3 l (3¼ quarts) of cold water. Bring to the boil, skim and cook over low heat for 1 hour.
4. Meanwhile soak the dried mushrooms in 300 ml (1¼ cups) of warm water for 20 minutes. Squeeze them and chop them. Strain the mushroom water through a sieve and keep it.
5. In a frying pan, heat the oil and gently fry the pancetta, onion, celery and carrot over low heat for 10 minutes, until soft.
6. Add the mushrooms, tomato sauce and chilli and cook for 5 minutes.
7. Pour the mixture into the pot of legumes, together with the cabbage, the lentils and the strained mushroom water.
8. Cook for another 45 minutes-1 hour over low heat, until all the legumes are tender. If the soup gets too thick, add hot water.
9. Add salt and the pasta. Cook it in the soup for the time on the packet, stirring often so it doesn't stick.
10. Turn off the heat and leave for 5 minutes.
11. Serve with pecorino, a drizzle of raw olive oil and a grind of pepper.

## Tips
- The soup should be thick: the spoon should almost stand up.
- It is even better the next day. Add a little water when reheating.

## Common mistakes
- Don't use the soaking water for cooking.
- Don't eat grass peas often and in large amounts: they contain a substance that, eaten over long periods, can damage the nerves. Soak them well and discard the soaking water.

## Local variations
- Without pancetta, with just olive oil, for meatless days and Lent.
- With fresh Sila mushrooms instead of dried ones, in autumn.

## Storage
In the fridge for 3 days, without the pasta if possible: add it when reheating. In the freezer, without pasta, for 3 months.

## Substitutions
Grass peas are sold in health-food shops. You can replace them with more chickpeas or beans. Instead of dried porcini, fresh button mushrooms fried in the base work.

## Allergens (14 EU allergens)
Gluten (pasta), celery, milk (pecorino).

## PDO and PGI
Optional: **Pecorino Crotonese PDO**; a **Calabrian PDO** extra virgin olive oil.

## Wine
A **Terre di Cosenza DOC red**.
